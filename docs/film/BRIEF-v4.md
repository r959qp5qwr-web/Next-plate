# Next Plate 30 s film: brief from the owner's survey (2026-11-12)

| Item | Owner's answer |
|---|---|
| Audience | Both: current clients and new people |
| Their need | A diet plan for weight loss; guidance on nutrition issues: vitamin deficiency, iron deficiency, diabetes |
| Where watched | WhatsApp forward |
| What they should take away | Reliable meal recommendations for my goals and needs |
| Health needs named | All: weight loss, iron and vitamin gaps, diabetes, protein |
| Feature that must appear | Your targets, worked out; it must feel like a useful personal diet planner |
| Opening | The app, straight away |
| Message direction | Personal diet planner (closest), in more relatable words |
| Tone | Warm, conversational |
| Voice and sound | The owner's voice; gentle music (soft piano); taps; a chime when a card arrives |
| Language | English |
| Length, visuals, pace | About 30 s; app only; calm |
| Line style | Plain statement |
| Ending | Name only; later extended by the owner to the brand line "Next Plate. Made for you." |

## Approved script
1. Next Plate plans your meals for your goal and your health.
2. Tell it your age, height, weight and goal. It works out what you need each day, and shows you how.
3. Then, at every meal, it gives you one plate. What to eat, and how much, in grams.
4. Losing weight, low on iron, or managing diabetes, each plate is planned around it.
5. Next Plate. Made for you. (the close line chosen by the owner after the option rounds; spoken and shown)

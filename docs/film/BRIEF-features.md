# Next Plate features film (60 s): brief, gate 1

Owner's survey (2026-11-12): one 60 s film; all four feature groups; for clients who saw the 30 s film; same voice and look as the 30 s film.

| # | Line | Brief |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Audience | Clients who have seen the 30 s film and know what Next Plate is. They watch on the phone in WhatsApp, often with sound on (the 30 s film is voiced), but the film must also read with sound off. |
| 2 | Insight | A diet plan is written once. The day doesn't follow it: a meal is skipped, lunch is eaten out, a craving comes at six. (From the owner's own draft post: "Most diet plans are written once and handed over. Your day doesn't stay that fixed.") Owner chose this (2026-11-12). |
| 3 | Category convention (to avoid) | Before and after bodies, calorie counting and guilt, celebrities and user stories (research notes, Noom and HealthifyMe); fast feature montages with upbeat music and an animated voice (the owner rejected this in promo v2). |
| 4 | Single-minded proposition | Whatever your day does, Next Plate plans the next plate around it. Owner chose this (2026-11-12). |
| 5 | Reasons to believe (on screen, the app's own screens) | (a) You skip or log a meal, and the next plate's target changes. (b) Eating out: type the dish and see how it fits your day and what to do next (Rescue), or photograph a menu and get one order for you (Read the menu). (c) Ask for a craving or what's in the fridge and get a plate for it, then change it in one tap: more protein, gentler on sugar, 10-minute version, easy on the gut. (d) Builder: set your own plate ratio and see the after-meal curve: Gentle, Moderate or Sharp. |
| 6 | The purple cow | Read the menu: photograph a restaurant menu and it picks one order for you, with how much to eat and why. Who forwards it: a client who eats out for work, to a friend in the same spot. |
| 7 | Desired response | Reply "plate" for the link, as in the WhatsApp post. |
| 8 | Mandatories and bans | Owner's voice "Mine" (warm, natural pace); same piano score family; Raw Linen look and the revised plate (portion ring, nutrient colours, stoneware plate, Pair it, taste notes); the app's own screens only, captured with the live engine answering; grams and kcal, never katori or phulka; no supplements; no blood-sugar numbers (the curve is a band); no personas or invented insights; no banned words; no three-beat taglines or slogans in the owner's voice; ends on the name. |

## How the four groups fit in 60 s (to be settled at gate 2)
About 5 s to open on the app and the name, 7 to 9 s per feature for five or six features, about 6 s to close on the name and the reply. Eating out is the feature that gets the most time.

## Dependency for gate 4
The screens have to be captured with the live engine answering, and the Gemini prepaid credits are used up. Gates 1 to 3 can go ahead now. Capture waits until the owner tops up the credits.

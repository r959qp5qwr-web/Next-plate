# Next Plate features film (60 s): concepts, gate 2

Proposition (owner's pick): Whatever your day does, Next Plate plans the next plate around it.
Every concept uses the app's own screens, the owner's voice and the 30 s film's look, and ends on the name and "Reply plate".

## A. One day on the app (demonstration) — recommended
**Idea.** The film runs through one day on the app's own clock, morning to night. Each time the day goes off plan, the app answers it, and each answer is a feature.
**Beats.** 8:10 am, breakfast skipped (tap "Skipped it"), and the rest of the day adjusts (the app spreads a missed meal; no single meal makes up for it). 1:15 pm, lunch at a restaurant: photograph the menu and get one order (Read the menu). 6:00 pm, a craving: type "chaat", get a plate, then tap "Gentler on sugar". 8:30 pm, dinner planned around all of it, with the after-meal curve in Builder. Then the name.
**First 3 seconds.** The app's masthead and the time "8:10 am", and a finger taps "Skipped it". Voice: "Breakfast didn't happen."
**Ending.** Dinner's plate settles, the day's four meals are ticked, then NEXT PLATE and "Reply *plate* for your link."
**Score (1 to 5).** Insight true 5 · one proposition 5 · remarkable 4 · product is the hero 5 · readable on a phone 4 · brand early 4 · plain words 5 · one action 5 · owner's rules 5.

## B. The day says, the plate answers (contrast)
**Idea.** Each beat opens on a plain card naming what the day did, in four words or fewer ("Lunch at a wedding."), then cuts to the app answering it. The cards are the day and the screens are Next Plate.
**Beats.** "Skipped breakfast." → the targets move. "Lunch out." → Rescue, then the menu order. "Craving at six." → Ask, then one-tap changes. "Want more veg?" → Builder ratio and curve.
**First 3 seconds.** A paper card in the Raw Linen style: "Skipped breakfast." Then the app's plate answers.
**Ending.** A last card, "Whatever your day does.", then the name and "Reply plate".
**Score.** Insight true 5 · one proposition 5 · remarkable 3 · product is the hero 4 · readable 5 · brand early 3 · plain words 5 · one action 5 · owner's rules 5. Weaker on brand early and on remarkable: the cards take screen time away from the app.

## C. What people type (light humour)
**Idea.** The film is a run of the requests the app already offers as prompts: "Craving chaat at 6 pm", "Only eggs, spinach and bread at home", "What to order at a restaurant tonight", "Trek tomorrow: food to carry". The voice reads each one, deadpan, and the app answers with a plate.
**First 3 seconds.** The ask box, with "Only eggs, spinach and bread at home" being typed. Voice reads it.
**Ending.** The ask box empty and waiting, then the name and "Reply plate".
**Score.** Insight true 4 · one proposition 3 · remarkable 4 · product is the hero 5 · readable 4 · brand early 4 · plain words 5 · one action 5 · owner's rules 5. It shows Ask well, but the day re-planning and Builder fit only loosely, so it carries "ask it anything" more than the chosen proposition.

## Recommendation
A. It is the only concept where every feature serves the one thought, the day moving and the plate following, and it is all the app's own screens. Only A clears 4 on every line.

## Owner's decision
2026-11-12: A was picked first; its script was turned down ("No. Doesn't work."). The owner then chose **B, the day says, the plate answers**. B is fixed.

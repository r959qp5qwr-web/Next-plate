# Next Plate features film (60 s): script and storyboard, gate 3

Concept A, "One day on the app" (owner's pick). Proposition: Whatever your day does, Next Plate plans the next plate around it.
Voice: the owner's "Mine", warm storyteller, natural pace. About 125 words, roughly 50 s spoken, with room for pauses and the close.

## Narration (as spoken)
1. Breakfast didn't happen. That's fine. Next Plate spreads it over the rest of your day.
2. Lunch is at a restaurant. Take a photo of the menu. It picks one order for you: what to have, how much, and why.
3. At six, you want chaat. Ask for it. You get a plate that fits your day, in grams. One tap makes it gentler on sugar.
4. By dinner, it has your whole day. The plate is planned around it. Want more vegetables? Change the plate, and see if the rise after the meal is gentle or sharp.
5. Whatever your day does, Next Plate plans the next plate around it.
6. Reply "plate", and I'll send you your link.

## Checks against the brief
- Every voice line matches what the app does: a skipped meal is spread over the day ("No meal is asked to make up for it"); Read the menu takes a photo and returns one order with portion and reasons; Ask returns a plate in grams; "Gentler on sugar" is a one-tap change; Builder shows the after-meal band (Gentle, Moderate, Sharp), never a number.
- No personas or invented figures. Grams only. No supplements. No banned words. The proposition is said once, near the end.
- Brand early: the app's masthead NEXT PLATE is on screen from the first frame; the name is said at 2.5 s and at the close.
- Sound off: the words on screen and the clock carry the day. Sound on: the voice alone tells it.

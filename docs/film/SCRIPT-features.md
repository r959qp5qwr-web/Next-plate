# Next Plate features film (60 s): script and storyboard, gate 3

Script approved by the owner (2026-11-12: "No all good."). Gate 4 waits on Gemini credits.
Concept B, "The day says, the plate answers" (owner's pick, 2026-11-12, after the concept A script was turned down: "No. Doesn't work.").
Proposition: Whatever your day does, Next Plate plans the next plate around it.
Timings are the 51 s cut (owner: "a little lengthier than it should"; beats tightened, close as the 30 s film). Owner's change (2026-11-12): the opening "Skipped breakfast" beat replaced by "Heavy lunch" (skipping read as a loophole for weight loss and a risk on diabetes medicine).
Voice: the owner's "Mine", warm storyteller, natural pace. About 125 words.

## How it works on screen
Each beat opens on a paper card in the Raw Linen style (EB Garamond, linen paper, a little grain) naming what the day did, in four words or fewer. The card slides away and the app answers it on its own screen, with one detail lifted out, as in the 30 s film. The cards are the day; the screens are Next Plate.

## Narration (as spoken)
1. Lunch ran heavy? Next Plate plans dinner lighter, so your day still adds up.
2. Eating out? Take a photo of the menu. It picks one order for you, and how much to have.
3. Had something else? Type it in. It's counted, and the next plate adjusts.
4. A craving at six? Ask for it. You get a plate that fits your day, in grams.
5. One tap changes the plate: more protein, gentler on sugar, ready in ten minutes, or easy on the gut.
6. Want more vegetables? Set your own plate in Builder, and see if the rise after the meal is gentle or sharp.
7. Whatever your day does, Next Plate plans the next plate around it.
8. Next Plate. Made for you. (the brand line, the 30 s film's own read; "Reply plate for your link." is on screen only — owner, 2026-11-12: "next plate made for you has to be consistent. That's our brand slogan.")

## Checks against the brief
- Every voice line matches what the app does: after a heavy earlier meal the next plate is lighter (hook "lighter after earlier meals"; live-engine check heavy-lunch-lighter-dinner); Read the menu takes a photo and returns one order with a portion; "I had something else" counts a typed meal and the day re-plans; Ask returns a plate in grams; the four one-tap changes are the app's own; Builder shows the after-meal band (Gentle, Moderate, Sharp), never a number.
- No personas or invented figures. Grams only. No supplements. No banned words.
- Brand early: the masthead NEXT PLATE is on screen in the first beat and the name is said at about 3 s. Gate 2 marked B weaker on this; putting the app on screen under the first card fixes it.
- Sound off: the cards carry the day and the screens carry the answers. Sound on: the voice alone tells it.

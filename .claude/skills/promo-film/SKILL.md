# Promo film method

Work in four gates. Do not start a gate until the one before it is approved by the owner.

## Gate 1. The brief (one page)
Fill every line. If a line cannot be filled from evidence, stop and find it.
1. **Audience:** who, in one sentence, and the moment they are in when they see the film (phone, WhatsApp, sound off or on).
2. **Insight:** a human truth or tension, not a demographic. Test: would the audience say "that's me"? (Pandey: every idea starts from a truth people already live.)
3. **Category convention:** what every competitor's film says and shows. List it, so the film can avoid it.
4. **Single-minded proposition:** one thought, one sentence, in plain words. Not a feature list. (Bernbach: single-minded, not simple-minded. Ogilvy: say what is good about the product more clearly than anyone.)
5. **Reasons to believe:** 2 to 4 concrete proofs the product can show on screen. Proof, not adjectives.
6. **The purple cow:** the one thing about the product or the film that is worth telling someone else about. If nothing is remarkable, the film will be invisible (Godin). Name who would forward it, and why.
7. **Desired response:** what the viewer does next.
8. **Mandatories and bans:** owner's rules (words, units, safety lines, look).

## Gate 2. Concepts
Write three different concepts on one page each: a title, the idea in two sentences, how the first 3 seconds look, how it ends. Make them truly different (demonstration; contrast or enemy; human truth or humour). Score each against the checklist below and recommend one. The owner picks; the pick is fixed.

## Gate 3. Script and storyboard
- Write the narration as spoken words, then read it aloud. Cut every word that does not carry information.
- **Banned words** (they sound machine-written and say nothing): real, quietly, simply, seamlessly, effortlessly, exactly, just works, journey, unlock, empower, smart, magic, game-changer, revolutionary, transform, curated, tailored, personalised, holistic, wellness journey. No three-beat taglines. No slogans in the owner's voice.
- Order follows the proposition: the core use first, the remarkable feature where it does most work, safety where trust is needed, the call to action last.
- Storyboard every shot on one line: time, picture, words on screen, voice.

## Gate 4. Production checks (Google/Kantar ABCD, phone-first)
- **Attention:** the hook lands in the first 3 seconds, on screen and in sound. High contrast, readable at phone size. Every shot held long enough to read twice.
- **Branding:** the product name or the product on screen within the first 5 seconds, and again at the end.
- **Connection:** the viewer should feel something (relief, recognition, a laugh). Emotional campaigns outperform rational ones over time (Binet and Field, IPA).
- **Direction:** one clear call to action, written and spoken.
- **Demonstration:** the product is the hero (Ogilvy). Show the real product doing the thing. No invented screens.
- **Sound-off test:** the story reads with the sound off. **Sound-on test:** the voice alone tells it.

## Checklist to score a concept or a cut (1 to 5 each; ship only at 4+ on all)
Insight is true · one proposition · remarkable · product is the hero · readable on a phone · brand early · plain words · ends with one action · within the owner's rules.

Idea
The five inputs are five words in one sentence. Tap a word to change it.
Buys
The most compact input and the clearest statement of what is set. The answer gets the most room, as a readable list.
Costs
Words in running text are weak buttons for tired eyes, the sentence reflows when a value changes, and the dial shrinks to an ornament. Controls sit at the top, out of thumb reach.

One plate at a time, so comparing three needs swiping. Curved and corner labels drop to 11-13 px, and the food list has to be abbreviated to fit.

Recommendation: Study A, Console

The decisive tradeoff. A gives up some screen height to the dock so that turning any control changes an answer you can already see, with the controls where the thumb rests. B reads best but hides its buttons in prose. C looks best in a screenshot but shows one plate at a time and needs small text.

Carried into the build from the others. From B, each key states its current value in words, so defaults are apparent. From C, the plate drawing is the primary identifier of each option, and swiping between plates is kept as a shortcut beside the tabs.

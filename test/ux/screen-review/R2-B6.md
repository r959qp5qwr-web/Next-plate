## 081 Rescue: the answer to a craving (rescue-result)
Purpose: for a dish already chosen, show its energy and macros, the rise band and the moves the person can tick, then log it.

Verdict: 1 BLOCKER, 1 MAJOR, 1 MINOR, 2 COSMETIC.
1. BLOCKER (owner rule 3, with diabetes the grain stays; A14 gap). With diabetes, a Rescue move that drops the grain is shown. checkRescue
   filters moves for supplements, claims and food-rule crossings only (src/v5/core.js:587-590); it never calls removesGrain() or STAND, which
   the menu path applies to its portion and ask (src/v6/engine.js:562). The Rescue prompt carries the meal-plan diabetes line, not a rule for
   moves (core.js:602, 664-667). Probe on the built page, diabetes profile, lunch, typed "Paneer butter masala with 2 butter naan": the moves
   "Skip the naan, eat the paneer with salad" and "Leave out the naan completely" both pass the check and would be drawn as tickable moves
   under "Moves you can make". Dropping the grain is the first thing most models suggest for eating out, and for a client on sugar tablets
   it is the advice the owner's rule exists to stop. The screen as rendered (360-light/rescue-result-2.png) shows a safe move ("Set aside a
   third of the rice"), but nothing in the code holds that. No rule in test/rules/check.js covers Rescue moves and the grain
   (check.js:1140-1177 cover words and diet only).
   Smallest fix: in checkRescue, when the profile has diabetes and the meal is not a snack, drop any move whose title removesGrain() or names
   a stand-in (as engine.js:562 does for the menu), and say in the Rescue prompt "never a move that leaves out the roti, rice or other grain;
   a portion move may leave part of it". Add a rule check with these two planted moves.
2. MAJOR (owner rule 2, grams and kcal only). The Rescue portion and moves are not held to grams, and "katori" passes. The prompt asks for
   "portion (as usually served, at most 8 words)" with no grams and no ban on household units (core.js:664); the menu prompt asks for "pieces
   or grams ... No household units such as katori" (engine.js:363) and clears a portion that says katori (engine.js:558). Probe: a portion
   "2 katori curry and 2 naan" and a move "Have only half a katori of the gravy" both come back from the check unchanged. Rendered: the
   portion line reads "Full handi, as delivered" with no grams (360-light/rescue-result-1.png). The fixture text is MOCK-ONLY (B4 34-4), but
   the live prompt asks for the same shape, so live answers will carry no grams and may carry katori.
   Smallest fix: ask for the Rescue portion "in pieces or grams, no household units such as katori"; in checkRescue clear a portion that
   names katori and drop a move whose title does; add both to the rule checks.
3. MINOR (B1 hierarchy, B3 legibility). The screen never says whether this dish fits the day. The lede with "850 kcal and 66 g protein are
   left today" is hidden once an answer shows (app.js:620), so 1120 kcal stands alone, and the only judgement, "Close to your
   target: 20 / 100", is in the 15 px grey italic line at the bottom of the card (.tsub, legible.css:18; 360-light/rescue-result-1.png,
   360-light-big/rescue-result-2.png where it wraps to "20 / 100" alone). A 50-year-old reading at arm's length sees a big number and no
   verdict. Smallest fix: one line at body size under the dish name, from figures the app already holds: "About 1120 kcal. About 850 kcal
   are left for today."
4. COSMETIC (B5). The move markers are empty circles (shell.html:547), the look of radio buttons, though several moves can be ticked. A
   portion move reads "-310 kcal" with a hyphen while the others read "+65 kcal" (app.js:676). Fix: a rounded square marker, as on the
   habit tiles, or a caption "Tick any"; write the saving as "310 kcal less" or with a true minus sign.
5. COSMETIC (B2 grouping). The heading "Another menu or dish" sits as far from the controls it heads as from the card above it (about 45 px
   each side at 360: 360-light/rescue-result-2.png, rescue-refer-2.png, rescue-menu-order-3.png). Fix: halve the space under .again-h.
- Earlier findings: 34-1 FIXED (moves start off; "Moves you can make"); 34-2 FIXED (legend "As served, then with the moves you tick";
  the score wording is the owner's decision); 34-3 FIXED (the portion move that names mutton stays); 34-4 MOCK-ONLY, still visible ("Layout
  sample from your Gemini file, not AI output.", a mutton dish for a vegetarian); 34-5 fixed in the source (app.js row(): the arrow and the
  new figure share a nowrap span) but not rendered: no review state ticks a move.
Not inspected: the screen with a move ticked (bars, arrows, the score moving); a live Gemini answer; what "I had this" logs with moves ticked.

# Screen review, second pass, batch B7 (screens 097 to 112), build 9274da67

Reviewer: independent per-screen review. Method: every frame at 360-light, then 320-light and 360-light-big (130 % text), and a look at
360-dark. I read them against src/v6/app.js, src/v6/legible.css, src/v6/engine.js (menu note, moves, grain grams), test/ux/review.js
(how each state is reached), test/ux/atlas/names.js, test/ux/screen-review/B6.md and FIXES.md, and docs/ux/UX-CRITERIA.md (owner
decisions in part A and part D are not raised again). I did not run the review; the screenshots are the ones on disk for this build.
Screens 097 to 107 are new since the first pass and got the full method. Screens 108 to 112 are B6 screens 45 to 49; their earlier
findings are checked below.
On every screen in this batch, dark mode keeps the Raw Linen paper (A8, D2), I found no banned word, units are g and kcal, and every
stand-alone control is 44 px or more. Fixture text such as "heavy for a dinner" on a lunch, and "Gulab Jamun ... better shared on another
day", is MOCK-ONLY (test/v6/fixture-menu.js) and is not raised.
A finding that applies to several screens is counted once, on the first screen where it appears, and referred to on the others.

## 097 Menu: another order picked (menu-other-order)
Purpose: after the person taps an order under "Also on this menu", that order becomes "Your order" with its figures and "I'll order this".

Verdict: 3 MINOR, 2 COSMETIC.
1. MINOR (B3, legible at arm's length; B9, one look). The paragraph under the order is 15 px grey italic and six lines long. It holds two
   things the person needs: "2 dishes on this menu did not pass your food rules, so they are not shown" and the AI notice. On set-up and
   Plate, the disclaimer and the Gemini line were moved to upright 16 px ink2 (class `fine disc`, legible.css:250; FIXES B1 01-4); this one
   was not. Evidence: 360-light/menu-other-order-3.png and -4.png, 360-light-big/menu-other-order-3.png; source app.js:608
   `el("p","fine",...)`, legible.css:17. Smallest fix: give this paragraph the class `fine disc`. Applies to 098, 099, 108, 110 and 112.
2. MINOR (B7, plain words; B9). The third tile on the order card reads "Rise" with "Moderate" under it. On Plate the same tile was renamed
   "Sugar rise" because "Rise" alone was unexplained (FIXES B1 06-3; app.js:333). The menu card kept "Rise" (app.js:568
   `cell("Rise",band,...)`). Evidence: 360-light/menu-other-order-1.png. Smallest fix: `cell("Sugar rise",...)`. Applies to every order
   card in this batch (098, 099, 108, 110, 112).
3. MINOR (B5, controls look tappable; A9 spirit). At 130 % text the first "common dish" chip, "Paneer butter masala with 2 butter naan",
   is wider than the screen. Its last word is cut at the right edge and no second chip shows, so nothing tells the person the row scrolls
   sideways (the scrollbar is hidden: `.sits` is `overflow-x:auto; scrollbar-width:none`, src/v5/extra.css:47 with the shell rule).
   Evidence: 360-light-big/menu-other-order-4.png and -5.png; the same at 130 % on 099 to 112. At normal text the second chip peeks in,
   which is enough (360-light/menu-other-order-4.png). Smallest fix: let `.sits` wrap at large text, or cap a chip at about 85 % of the row
   width so the next chip always shows.
4. COSMETIC (B2). At 320 px the separator dots break badly: "Planned around: protein to catch up ·" ends its line with the dot, and the
   alternative lines start with "· ≈ 28 g protein" (320-light/menu-other-order-2.png). Fix: join each " · " to the item after it with a
   no-break space.
5. COSMETIC (B2, spacing in steps). "Another menu or dish" sits about 55 px below the paragraph above and about 55 px above the group it
   heads, so it does not read as that group's heading (360-light/menu-other-order-3.png). Fix: halve the space under `.again-h`.
- Good: the picked order is scrolled to the top of the view with its name, grams and the one main button, "I'll order this", in thumb reach
  after one scroll. The better-scoring order (Paneer Tikka, 92 / 100) stays listed under "Also on this menu" with its score.
Not inspected: what a screen reader hears after the pick; 390 and 412 px.

## 098 Menu: the closest order, with a move (menu-closest)
Purpose: when nothing on the menu fits, a note says so, the closest order is shown, and the person can tick moves ("Eat about half and
pack the rest", "A 15-minute walk after the meal"). The screen shows the state after ticking the half-portion move.

Verdict: 1 MAJOR, 1 COSMETIC (plus 097-1, 097-2, 097-3).
1. MAJOR (B6 truthful; owner rule 5 spirit, a figure must match what it describes). With "Eat about half" ticked, the card still says
   "1 bowl, ≈ 300 g" and "with 2 Butter Naan", but its Energy and Protein tiles now show half: "≈ 575 kcal", "≈ 15 g" (served: 1150 kcal,
   30 g). Rise changes from the served "Sharp" to "Moderate". Nothing on the card says these figures are for half. Only the score chip shows
   the change ("22 → 57 / 100"). The note above still says "As served it runs ≈ 500 kcal over". A client reading the card sees a full bowl
   with two naan at 575 kcal. "I'll order this" then logs 575 kcal (app.js:572 logs `a`, the scaled figures). If they eat the whole
   order, the day is about 575 kcal short. Rescue shows both figures ("As served, then with the moves you tick"); this card does not.
   Evidence: 360-light/menu-closest-1.png and -2.png, 320-light/menu-closest-2.png; source app.js:552-553 (`a=scaleN(a,m.scale)`), :568
   (tiles from `a`), :563 (portion line unchanged). Smallest fix: show the change in each figure, as the score already does ("≈ 1150 → 575
   kcal", "Sharp → Moderate"). Then add "(eating half)" after the portion line while a portion move is ticked.
2. COSMETIC (B7). "over what this lunch should carry" (engine.js:529-530) is an odd verb for these readers. Fix: "more than this lunch
   should have".
- Good: the note leads, on the info style (no error rule), and after the tick it is scrolled back into view (FIXES "menu-closest" item).
  The ticked move has a filled check and a clay border; the unticked one is plainly off. "The two moves that help most" is true: menuMoves
  always returns two (engine.js:517-526).
Not inspected: the card with the walk ticked; a diabetes profile on this state (it uses the no-grain seed).

## 45 Menu, diabetes: grain available (Yes) (menu-grain-yes)
Purpose: after the person answers Yes to "Is tandoori roti available now?", the order card shows the grain confirmed and the button "I'll order this".

Verdict: 3 MINOR, 1 COSMETIC.
1. MINOR (B5, one primary action). There are two filled clay buttons: "Read the menu" at the top of Rescue stays primary while the order card
   shows "I'll order this". From the top of the page the first read is "Read the menu", not the order (360-light/menu-grain-yes-1.png: the order
   card starts at the fold; 320-light/menu-grain-yes-1.png: the order card is not visible at all). Source: app.js:544
   `btn(UI.mopen?"btn":"btn primary","Read the menu",...)`, which ignores whether an order is on screen.
   Smallest fix: make "Read the menu" a secondary button while `UI.mres` is shown. The same applies to screens 47, 49, 50 and 51, where the
   card's only buttons are the outlined Yes and No, so the strongest control on the page is the one that leaves the order.
2. MINOR (owner rule 2, numbers in g and kcal). The grain the plan keeps is shown as a count with no grams: "with 1 Tandoori Roti"
   (360-light/menu-grain-yes-2.png), and "with 2 Tandoori Roti" under "Also on this menu" (menu-grain-yes-3.png). The engine already holds the
   grain's grams (`grain_grams`, 40 g for one roti in test/v6/fixture-menu.js:12; required with diabetes, engine.js:556), but the card does not
   show them. The main dish gets grams ("6 pieces, ≈ 180 g") and the grain does not. For a client with diabetes, the grain amount is the number
   that matters most. Rice is worse: "with 1 Steamed Rice" (screen 47) and "with Steamed Rice and 1 Roti" (screen 51) give no amount at all.
   Smallest fix: pass `grain_grams` through to the order and show it after the side, for example "with 1 Tandoori Roti, ≈ 40 g". This needs the
   owner's ruling on whether a restaurant count such as "1 roti" counts as a phulka-style unit under rule 2.
3. MINOR (B3, legible at arm's length; B1, hierarchy). The only visible record of the safety answer, "Tandoori roti: available, you checked.",
   uses the faintest style on the card: 15 px italic in the fine-print grey (legible.css:17 `.fine`, used by app.js:518), below the olive
   "planned around" line and the clay "Ask:" line (360-light/menu-grain-yes-2.png). The same style carries "You said tandoori roti is not
   available." on screen 47. Smallest fix: set `.gok` and `.gno` upright at 16 to 17 px in ink, with the check or cross icon.
4. COSMETIC (B9, one look). Here "Change answer" has no underline and starts 4 px to the right of the line above. On screens 46 and 47 the same
   link is underlined and lines up. Source: legible.css:69 (`.linkbtn` padding 0 4px); the underline and zero padding are set only for
   `.note .linkbtn` and `.gno .linkbtn` (legible.css:240-241), not for `.gok`. Fix: give `.order .gok .linkbtn` the same rule.
- Fixture note (not a product finding): "Malai Kofta ... heavy for a dinner." sits under "Your order · lunch". This is the review fixture's
  text (fixture-menu.js:28); live text comes from Gemini.
Not inspected: live Gemini wording of the side and portion; what a screen reader hears after Yes; 390 and 412 px; the physical phone.

## 46 Menu, diabetes: grain not available (No) (menu-grain-no)
Purpose: after No to the only grain on offer, the order list gives way to a note that says no order is left and what to do instead.

Verdict: 2 MINOR.
1. MINOR (B6, truthful next step; B7). The note says "every order on this menu needs it". That is not true of the menu the person typed: it sells
   Jeera Rice and Veg Biryani (fixture-menu.js:8). The app keeps only the best three orders (docs/FEATURE-menu.md:39), all with tandoori roti,
   and a No filters only those three (app.js:472-474). The note then tells the person to "Ask which roti or rice they have", when the rice is on
   the menu in their hand. Smallest fix: say what is true, for example "every order Next Plate picked from this menu needs it". Better still,
   have the server return the next-ranked orders, so a No can fall back to a dish with another grain.
2. MINOR (B6, next step in view; B5). The next step the note names, the box "Already chosen? Name the dish", is above the note. When focus
   moves to the note (app.js:474, scrolled to the top of the view), that box is off screen at 320 px and at 130 % text
   (320-light/menu-grain-no-2.png, 360-light-big/menu-grain-no-2.png). Below the note, the old intro line still reads "Before you order, read the
   menu ..." (360-light/menu-grain-no-2.png), and "Read the menu" is still the primary button. Both invite reading the same menu again, which
   brings back the same note (screen 48). Smallest fix: hide the intro line while this note is shown, and give the note a "Name the dish" button
   that focuses #rin.
Not inspected: the live-region announcement; the menu text box after a No (it is closed and emptied, app.js:455 and :469, so a typed menu must
be typed again); 390 and 412 px.

## 51 Menu, diabetes: two grains in one order (menu-grain-two)
Purpose: an order with steamed rice and roti asks about each grain in turn. The screens show the second question, "Is roti available now?",
after Yes to rice.

Verdict: 2 MINOR (plus 45-1, 45-2 and 47-1).
1. MINOR (B5, state visible; IX feedback). After Yes to steamed rice, nothing on screen records that answer. The box keeps its place and size,
   and one word changes ("Is roti available now?"; 360-light/menu-grain-two-2.png). The "available, you checked" line appears only when no grain
   is pending (app.js:510 and 518). There is no "1 of 2", and the rice answer cannot be changed until roti is answered. Yes stays under the same
   finger, so a quick double tap answers both questions Yes. Smallest fix: while a second grain is pending, show "Steamed rice: available, you
   checked." above the second question, and title the box "Next: is roti available now?".
2. MINOR (B6, truthful; from the source, not rendered). If the answer is Yes to rice and then No to roti, the order is dropped, although rice is
   confirmed: `grainGone` drops an order when any of its grains is refused (app.js:464, 473). With the fixture's other order also needing roti,
   the person then gets screen 46's note: "every order on this menu needs it. Ask which roti or rice they have", straight after saying rice is
   available. Smallest fix: add a review state for Yes-then-No, and when a Yes is on record, name it in the note (for example "You said steamed
   rice is available, but roti is not ..."). Or offer the order again with only the confirmed grain, if the owner agrees.
- Applies here: 45-2 ("with Steamed Rice and 1 Roti": the rice has no amount).
Not inspected: the Yes-then-No path on screen (no review state); the second grain's focus after Yes (app.js:515).

## 52 Plate: kidney condition, referred (plate-blocked-kidney)
Purpose: with a kidney condition set, Plate plans nothing and refers the person to their coach and doctor.

Verdict: 3 MINOR. The safety rule holds: no plate, no figure and no suggestion (app.js:337), on every width and scheme.
1. MINOR (B7, plain words; B6). The heading reads "This one is for your coach and doctor to plan with you." (SAFETY, core.js:87). "This one"
   has nothing to point to on a whole tab: the line was written as a reply to a single request. The body then repeats "coach and doctor"
   (360-light/plate-blocked-kidney-1.png). Smallest fix: give blockedCard its own heading, for example "Plan your meals with your coach and
   doctor", and keep SAFETY for the server's refer replies.
2. MINOR (B6, what to do next). For a person who does have the condition, the screen gives no step to take. The one button, "Change my
   answers" (app.js:188), is for a mis-tap, and as the only action it can nudge a person to untick the condition to get plates. Smallest fix:
   add a plain line with the next step, for example "Send your coach a WhatsApp message to plan your meals together", and keep "Change my
   answers" as the secondary way out for a mis-tap. The wording of the referral is the owner's to approve.
3. MINOR (B5, friction). "Change my answers" opens My food at the top (app.js:607). The health question comes after the goal, the food, the
   leave-outs and the home food (app.js:647), so the person must scroll about two screens to find what this card refers to (compare
   360-light/sheet-my-food-1..4.png). Smallest fix: when the sheet is opened from this card, scroll it to "Anything on the health side?" and
   focus the selected tile.
- Layout: holds at 320 px, at 130 % (the heading wraps to three lines and stays clear) and in dark mode. The button is 44 px or more.
Not inspected: the My food sheet as opened from this card; the screen reader's reading order; the owner's phone.

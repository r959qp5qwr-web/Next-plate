# Screen review B3: screens 17 to 28 (build 0b4f9482)

Method: I looked at every 360-light frame of each screen, then its 320-light and 360-light-big frames, and glanced at 360-dark. I read the source in src/v6/app.js (sheets at lines 603-659, plate states at 335-377, errors at 57-90) and src/v5/markup.html, and checked how test/ux/review.js reaches each state.
Paths: screenshots are under test/ux/out/screens/. Contact sheets I built for viewing are in /tmp/claude-0/-workspace/c6908978-4848-528a-96a3-1587c1886f9d/scratchpad/m/ and are not evidence.
Fixture text: "Layout sample, not AI output" (360-light/sheet-log-estimate-1.png) is the review's stand-in reply, not app copy. I did not judge it.
Dark mode: in all 12 screens, 360-dark shows the same paper palette as 360-light. No dark-mode findings.

## 17 Sheet: my food (sheet-my-food)
Purpose: change the set-up answers (goal, food, leave-outs, health, body) and see what data leaves the phone.
Findings:
1. BLOCKER: changes apply without new plates when the sheet is closed any way but Done. Only Done clears the saved plates (app.js:645). Tapping the dimmed band above the sheet (app.js:659) or pressing Escape (app.js:609) calls closeSheet(), which re-renders nothing and keeps STORE.res/STORE.shown (app.js:608). The answers already changed in memory (P.leave, P.diet, P.health are set on tap, app.js:146-157). The cached plate key does not include diet, leave-outs or health (ctxKey, app.js:49), so on the next render the old plate stays and the chips read the new answers (heroCard, app.js:261-262).
   - Concrete case: the review's own plate has "Walnut-mint chutney 30 g" (360-light/plate-ask-busy-3.png). Tap Nuts under "Leave out completely", tap the grey band, and the plate shows "No nuts" over walnut chutney.
   - Same with Diabetes: the line "With diabetes the grain stays on the plate" sits over a plate that was never checked for the grain.
   - Edits are also not saved (no save() in again(), app.js:646), so they are lost if the tab closes.
   - Not visible in any captured frame: this comes from the source.
   - Fix: run the Done handler on every way out of the "food" sheet (scrim, Escape), or apply and save on each change. Add a review state: change a leave-out, close by the scrim, assert the plate is re-planned.
2. MAJOR: Done throws away today's plates even when nothing changed. Done always runs STORE.res={}; STORE.shown={} (app.js:645). Done is the only clay button on the sheet, so it is also how people close it after just looking. Every look therefore costs a new Gemini request. Near the daily limit, it also deletes the plates the person had: plate-limit then has no "Show my last plates", because lastForMeal finds nothing (app.js:349). Fix: clear plates only when an answer changed (compare P before and after), and never delete the last plates before the new ones arrive.
3. MINOR: Done is out of reach. It sits only in the sheet header (360-light/sheet-my-food-1.png) and scrolls away; frames 2-4 have no way to finish. That is a long thumb reach on a 4-screen sheet. Fix: a sticky header, or a second Done at the foot.
4. MINOR: Height and Weight lose their units once filled. Labels "Age / Height / Weight"; the units exist only as placeholders (app.js:163-164), so the filled fields read "47 / 158 / 72" with no cm or kg (360-light/sheet-my-food-3.png). Many clients know height in feet: "5.2" is outside 120-215 and is cleared silently (app.js:165). Fix: label "Height (cm)" and "Weight (kg)", and say why a value was cleared.
5. MINOR: "Calcium and D" is shorthand (360-light/sheet-my-food-2.png). Fix: "Calcium and vitamin D".
6. MINOR: the data paragraph is one 50-word sentence ("Each time Next Plate plans a plate (also when you open it), counts a meal…", 360-light/sheet-my-food-3.png). It is hard to follow at arm's length. Fix: two short sentences, or a list of what is sent.
7. COSMETIC: the sheet title "My food" (weight 500) is lighter than its own section headings, which are bold (360-light/sheet-my-food-1.png). The first read goes to "What are you working towards?".
8. COSMETIC: the "Activity" caption sits about 10 px under the inputs, tighter than the gap above "Age" (360-light/sheet-my-food-3.png).
9. COSMETIC: at 320 px the sheet's top edge cuts through the "Vegetarian · North" line behind it (320-light/sheet-my-food-1.png). At 360 px with 130 % text it touches "your daily plate, journalled" (360-light-big/sheet-my-food-1.png).
Not inspected: the scrim and Escape path rendered (no state exists); Android back gesture (no history handling; it likely leaves the page); entering feet/inches on an actual keyboard.

## 18 Sheet: remove my data, confirm (sheet-data-confirm)
Purpose: confirm removing everything kept on this phone.
Findings:
1. MAJOR: the question leaves out the coach's link. It says "Remove your answers, your food record and today's plates from this phone?" (app.js:652). Removing also deletes the coach's code: it is stored inside np6 (app.js:35, k), np6 is removed, and the page reloads without the code (app.js:655). The person then does set-up again (three steps) and only then meets "Open the link your coach sent you on WhatsApp once. After that, this phone remembers it." (screen 28), which reads as if the phone forgot for no reason. Fix: add "…and the code from your coach's link. Open their WhatsApp link again afterwards." Or keep the code.
2. MINOR: there is no word after removal. location.replace drops the person on set-up step 1 with no line saying the data was removed (app.js:655). Fix: a one-line note on set-up after a removal.
3. COSMETIC: the pink box and a new brick red (360-light/sheet-data-confirm-1.png) step outside the linen palette. Acceptable for a destructive action, but it is the only pink surface in the batch.
Good: Keep and Remove carry equal size, focus lands on Keep, and "This cannot be undone." is stated.
Not inspected: the confirm at 130 % text. 360-light-big/sheet-data-confirm-1.png shows the question below the fold, because review.js enlarges text after scrolling the question into view (review.js:300 runs after go()) and takes one frame only (review.js:306). That frame is a capture artefact, not proof either way.

## 19 Sheet: how the numbers are worked out (sheet-how-numbers)
Purpose: explain where the day's kcal, protein and fibre targets come from.
Findings:
1. MINOR: the text says something the app cannot do. "Your coach can change them." (app.js:623). Nothing in src/v6 lets a coach change targets: params.js computes them from the answers alone, and there is no override field. A client who asks their coach will find the coach cannot. Fix: "Ask your coach if they look wrong for you", or remove the sentence.
2. MINOR: the sheet never states the result as a headline. It opens with "Maintenance. About 1700 kcal…" and buries the daily target inside "Weight loss… Never under 1200 kcal a day" (360-light/sheet-how-numbers-1.png). Fix: lead with one line, "Your day: about 1200 kcal and 75 g protein", then the reasons.
3. MINOR: jargon for this audience: "Mifflin-St Jeor formula", "times 1.3", "maintenance", "BMI 25", "ICMR-NIN", "1.2 g per kg" (params.js:59-82). Fix: keep the sources in brackets at the end of each note; lead with the plain reason.
4. COSMETIC: green check-circles beside explanations (360-light/sheet-how-numbers-1.png) read as "passed" ticks. Fix: plain bullets.
5. COSMETIC: at 360 px the title breaks with "out" alone on line 2 (360-light/sheet-how-numbers-1.png).
6. COSMETIC: "so the target stays realistic" (params.js:79) sits close to one of the banned words. Fix: "within reach".
Not inspected: the sheet for a man, someone over 65, someone on diabetes medicine, or the BMI-under-18.5 note (only one profile is captured).

## 20 Sheet: I had something else, typing (sheet-log-ask)
Purpose: type a meal eaten instead of the plate, to get it counted.
Findings:
1. MINOR: the start of the meal is hidden. At 320 px and at 130 % text, the one-line box shows only "oo paratha with curd and pickle" (320-light/sheet-log-ask-1.png, 360-light-big/sheet-log-ask-1.png). The person cannot check what they typed. Fix: a textarea that grows to two or three lines.
2. MINOR: the send control is an arrow with no word. Its name "Estimate it" exists only for screen readers (app.js:629). Fix: a visible word ("Count it") or a labelled button.
3. COSMETIC: typed text is italic like the hint, so typed and untyped differ only by ink darkness (360-light/sheet-log-ask-1.png).
4. COSMETIC: frames 1 and 2 are identical (the sheet does not scroll). Not a defect.
Good: the Gemini line is present where the text is typed (B12), the wording is plain, and the box and send are within thumb reach.
Not inspected: the "Working out what that adds up to" wait line (no state); the on-screen keyboard over the sheet.

## 21 Sheet: I had something else, the estimate (sheet-log-estimate)
Purpose: show the estimate of what was eaten and let the person accept or correct it.
Findings:
1. MINOR: the totals are the smallest text on the sheet. "≈ 640 kcal · 17 g protein · 84 g carbs · 26 g fats · 8 g fibre (an estimate)" is italic grey at 15 px (.tsub, extra.css:13), while the grams per food are large (360-light/sheet-log-estimate-1.png). The kcal total is what the day's target follows. Fix: give kcal a figure-size line of its own and keep the rest as the sub-line.
2. MINOR: "Not quite" does not say what happens. It returns to the box with the words kept (app.js:641). Fix: "Change it".
Good: grams and kcal with units on every figure, non-breaking units hold at 320 and 130 %, and "Yes, count it" is primary and in thumb reach.
Not inspected: live model text (the assumed line, food names); the toast after counting.

## 22 Sheet: I had something else, service busy (sheet-log-busy)
Purpose: tell the person the meal was not counted because the service is busy.
Findings:
1. MINOR: after the error the code focuses the text box (app.js:636 runs on every re-render without a result). On a phone that raises the keyboard over the note and Try again, the same problem round 30 fixed for the menu. The note is not sent to the live region either. Fix: after an error, focus the note (or Try again) and write it to #said.
2. COSMETIC: "Try again in a minute" sits beside a Try again button that works now, and the clay arrow does the same thing more prominently (360-light/sheet-log-busy-1.png). Fix: drop "in a minute", or let the note's Try again be the one primary.
Good: the typed words stay in the box (A13); the wording is plain.
Not inspected: the keyboard behaviour on a physical phone.

## 25 Plate: today's limit reached (plate-limit)
Purpose: tell the person no new plates can be made until the reset time.
Findings:
1. MINOR: requests that cannot succeed stay live. The four prompt cards and the clay send button remain the most visible controls (360-light/plate-limit-2.png, -3.png), and each fails with the same limit. Fix: while the limit holds, say under the composer that asks open again at the same time, or dim the send button.
2. MINOR: with no plates left there is nothing to do for this meal. The card offers no next step. The directive below already holds the meal's target ("Lunch carries about 480 kcal and 31 g protein"). Fix: point to it from the card, or to Builder if it can work without a plate.
3. COSMETIC: "today's limit" does not say why there is one. A first-timer may think they did something wrong.
Good: no Try again (A13); the time is given in the phone's clock ("5:30 am").
Not inspected: limit_ip wording ("on this phone").

## 26 Plate: asked while the service is busy (plate-ask-busy)

Good: the ask stays in the box and Try again is present (A13).
Not inspected: the network wording.

## 28 Plate: opened without the coach's link (plate-no-code)
Purpose: tell the person the app needs the coach's WhatsApp link once.
Findings:
1. MINOR: a new person meets the lock only after set-up. renderSetup never checks for the code (app.js:169-184). Someone who types nextplate.in answers all three set-up steps (health and body included) before this message. The review reaches it with a finished profile (review.js:58). Fix: show the same message before set-up when there is no code.
2. MINOR: prompts, composer, Builder and Rescue stay live and all fail the same way (360-light/plate-no-code-2.png). Fix: as in screen 25.
The wording itself is the owner's decision (14 Nov); it is not re-judged.
Not inspected: whether WhatsApp's in-app browser and the phone's browser share storage (the "this phone remembers it" promise); a wrong rather than missing code.

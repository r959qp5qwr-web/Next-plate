## 037 Plate: the last meal of the day logged (plate-last-meal)
Purpose: show the day closed after the last meal is logged.
Verdict:
1. MAJOR. The card and the day note say the day is over while lunch and snack are still open. The review logs dinner while lunch is the
   current meal (review.js:196). The Today note reads "So far 910 kcal and 47 g protein. That is every meal for today."
   (320-light/plate-last-meal-2.png; engine.js:142) and the card offers only "Reopen dinner", no "Plan lunch"
   (360-light/plate-last-meal-1.png). The meal tabs show Lunch with the "now" dot and no tick. Cause: nextOpen() only looks at meals after
   the current one (app.js:132), so after dinner it always returns none and directiveCard() sets done (app.js:242). A client who logs
   dinner early, or taps the wrong tab, is told to stop eating for the day with 290 kcal of the target not planned; for a client on
   insulin or sugar tablets that reads as permission to miss lunch. Fix: nextOpen() falls back to the first earlier main meal still open;
   "That is every meal for today" only when every slot is logged or skipped. Add a review guard for this state.
2. COSMETIC. Screen book route "Dinner, I had this." (names.js:81); the button is "I'll have this".
Not inspected: the screen when all four meals are in fact logged (no review state holds it).

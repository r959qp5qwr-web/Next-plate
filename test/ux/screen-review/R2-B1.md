# Screen review, second pass, batch B1 (screens 001 to 016), build 9274da67

Method: one screen at a time. Every frame at 360-light, then 320-light and 360-light-big (130 % text), with a look at 360-dark. Each screen
was checked against src/v6/app.js, src/v5/markup.html, src/v6/legible.css (plus src/v6/engine.js where the text comes from), against
docs/ux/UX-CRITERIA.md part B, and against the first-pass findings in B1.md, B2.md and FIXES.md. Paths below are under test/ux/out/screens/.
The review was not re-run. test/ux/out/report.md for this build reports 122 states, 1098 checks and no violations.
Dark mode: every frame of all 16 states at 360-dark is pixel-identical to 360-light (checked by pixel diff), so the paper look holds (A8, D2).
Words: no banned word, no katori or phulka unit and no supplement appears in src/v6/app.js or src/v5/markup.html (grep).
Dish names, Pair it lines, taste words and "Why this plate" come from the mock (test/v5/fixture.js) and are not judged as content.
Two live probes were run with playwright against hosted/v6/index.html, using a stubbed /api in the scratchpad. They only read and render;
no project file was changed. Probe 1 opened My food while the first plate was loading. Probe 2 tapped the Appetite pill.

## 011 Plate: making the plate (plate-loading)

Purpose: show that this meal's plates are being made, and how long it can take.

First-pass fixes checked:
- Fixed: asks are off with "Wait for this plate first." (360-light/plate-loading-3.png).
- Fixed: "It can take a minute or so."
- Fixed: "Planned around:" with a capital.
- Fixed: "Lunch: about 480 kcal and 31 g protein".
- Fixed: figures stay with their units at 130 % (360-light-big/plate-loading-2.png).

Verdict:
1. BLOCKER. A food rule changed while the plate is loading is dropped, and the plate made for the old rules is shown under the new rule's tick.
   - What happens:
     - My food (the "My food: Vegetarian · North" line) and the setting pills stay live during the wait (360-light/plate-loading-1.png, -2.png).
     - Closing My food after a change clears the stored plates (app.js:693).
     - The request already on its way still carries the old profile, and makePlates will not start another while one is running (app.js:92).
     - When the old request answers, its plates are stored and shown (app.js:97). Nothing asks again with the new answers.
   - Probe 1 setup: seeded vegetarian, no foods left out. While the first plate loaded, the probe opened My food, ticked Nuts and tapped Done.
   - Probe 1 result:
     - Only one /api/meals request was sent, with leave: [].
     - The plate then showed "✓ Vegetarian ✓ No nuts" over a plate whose "What to serve" lists "Walnut-mint chutney 30 g".
     - Screenshots: /tmp/claude-0/-workspace/c6908978-4848-528a-96a3-1587c1886f9d/scratchpad/probe/race.png and
       /tmp/claude-0/-workspace/c6908978-4848-528a-96a3-1587c1886f9d/scratchpad/probe/race-serve.png.
   - The same path drops a Diabetes tick: the plate then comes without the grain rule.
   - It also drops a goal, appetite or "More of" change made from the pills during the wait (changed(), app.js:262).
   - Why it matters: the first wait is the moment a new client is most likely to notice a missed answer and open My food. The plate then
     says the opposite of what it holds, and nuts are often left out for an allergy.
   - Criteria: B6, owner rule 3 (safety), B11.
   - Smallest fix: keep a counter or the profile sent with each request. When the answer comes back and the answers or settings have changed
     since (`answers()!==` the one sent), throw it away and call makePlates again. Add a review state that changes My food during plate-loading
     and asserts that the shown plate was requested with the new rules.
2. COSMETIC. At 130 % text the animated dots wrap onto a line of their own, under "Planning your lunch plates". FIXES (B4 33, B5 39) records
   this as fixed with `.lw{white-space:nowrap}`. Evidence: 360-light-big/plate-loading-1.png; legible.css:289-290. B2. Fix: put the dots
   inside the last word's box as text (a nowrap span holding the word and a fixed-width dots span), or let the last two words share the
   nowrap span.

Not inspected: switching the meal tab during the wait; the wait on an actual 4G phone; the animation itself (stills only).

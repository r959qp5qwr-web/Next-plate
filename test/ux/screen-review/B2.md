# Screen review, batch B2 (screens 09 to 16), build 0b4f9482

Reviewer: per-screen critic, batch B2. Method: every frame at 360-light first, then 320-light, then 360-light-big (130 % text), then a look at 360-dark.
Each screen was judged against the source (src/v6/app.js, src/v6/legible.css, src/v6/params.js, src/v5/core.js, src/v5/app.js for the shared toast) and docs/ux/UX-CRITERIA.md part B.
Screens are in test/ux/out/screens/<viewport>/<state>-<frame>.png. The review run was not repeated; the screenshots were taken as current for build 0b4f9482.
The plates on these screens come from the mock engine (test/v5/fixture.js, test/v5/mock-gemini.ts), so "Why this plate, today" and the asked read line show
"Layout sample ..." text. That text is a fixture and is not reported as a finding.

Dark mode: on all eight screens 360-dark matched 360-light frame for frame, with the same paper, inks and controls (B10 holds). Dark mode is not mentioned again below.
Banned words and units: the app strings behind these screens contain no banned word and no katori or phulka unit (checked by grep on src/v6/app.js and src/v5/markup.html).

## 09 Plate: no height, weight or age given (plate-no-body)

Purpose: the plate for a client who skipped the body step. No calorie or protein target is set, and the app asks them to add the missing details.

Verdict: findings.

1. BLOCKER. If the body step is skipped, the low-sugar warning for insulin and sugar tablets never appears.
   - What the code does: dayTargets6 returns null when age, height, weight, sex or activity is missing (src/v6/params.js:56). That makes PL null.
   - The skipped-meal warning on the card (src/v6/app.js:251) and in the toast (src/v6/app.js:318) are both inside `if(PL&&PL.day.meds)`.
   - Effect: a client who takes insulin or glimepiride, skipped the body step, and taps "Skipped it" sees only "The rest of the day adjusts. No meal is asked to make up for it in one sitting." The warning does not show.
   - Set-up lets clients skip this step: on step 3 the Next button is always enabled (src/v6/app.js:178).
   - The medicine question exists for this warning. It is the only safety line for this case.
   - No review state covers the combination: plate-skipped-meds seeds body data, and plate-no-body seeds meds "no".
   - Smallest fix: test `P.health==="diabetes"&&(P.meds==="yes"||P.meds==="unsure")` at app.js:251 and app.js:318, not `PL.day.meds`. Add a review state "skipped, on medicines, no body data" that asserts the warning shows.
   - Criteria: B6, owner rule 3 (safety).
2. MINOR. The only sign that this plate is not sized for the client is at frame 5 of 7.
   - Evidence: 360-light/plate-no-body-5.png ("Add your height and weight", under "Your day"). At 320 px it is frame 7 of 9.
   - Frames 1 to 4 look like a normal plate with "560 kcal". The only difference is the missing "Fit" chip, and nothing explains why it is missing.
   - A first-time client takes the grams as theirs.
   - Fix: one plain line at the top of the plate card, such as "These portions are not yet sized for you. Add your age, height and weight." The "Add them now" link would sit next to it.
   - Criteria: B1, B6.
3. MINOR. "Add them now" opens My food at its top, on the goal question.
   - The Age, Height and Weight boxes are on the third screenful of that sheet (360-light/sheet-my-food-3.png, src/v6/app.js:194 and app.js:647).
   - A client who taps the link sees goal and diet questions and may think it went to the wrong place.
   - Fix: when the sheet is opened from this link, scroll it to "For your targets" and focus Age.
   - Criterion: B6.
4. MINOR. The note asks only for "height and weight". Targets also need age, sex and activity (src/v6/params.js:56).
   - A client who adds height and weight but not age still has no targets, and the same note keeps asking for height and weight.
   - Fix: name what is actually missing, for example "Add your age, height and weight."
   - Criterion: B7, truthful copy.
5. MINOR, shared by every Plate screen and recorded here once. The suggested asks do not fit these clients.
   - They include "Heavy leg day: a high-protein dinner" and "Trek tomorrow: food to carry" (src/v6/app.js:321; 360-light/plate-no-body-6.png).
   - The clients are middle-aged, sedentary and pain-prone, and this one has "Desk day" set. Gym and trek prompts read as written for someone else.
   - Fix: ask the owner for prompts drawn from his clients' days, such as "Guests at home tonight" or "Tired, something quick". The text is the owner's choice and should not be invented here.
   - Criterion: B7.

Not inspected:
- Whether the server holds steady grain portions for a client on medicines when no body data is sent.
- Live Gemini text.
- A screen reader's reading of the note.

## 10 Plate: appetite dial open (plate-dial-open)

Purpose: the client says how hungry they are for this meal, 1 to 10, and that moves the meal's calorie target.

Verdict: findings.

1. MINOR. The pill that was tapped scrolls out of view, and the open panel has no name.
   - render() rebuilds the pill row, so it returns to its start. The "Appetite 6/10" pill is off the right edge, shown only as a dark sliver (360-light/plate-dial-open-6.png, right edge at the pill row).
   - That pill is also the only control that closes the panel.
   - The panel itself does not say "appetite" or "hunger". It shows only "A little … 6 of 10 · about 480 kcal … Very hungry".
   - Fix: keep the row's scroll position, or scroll the open pill into view. Add a caption above the slider: "How hungry are you for lunch?"
   - Criteria: B5, B6.
2. MINOR. Moving the slider changes nothing on the plate, and no next step is offered.
   - The change handler saves and re-renders (src/v6/app.js:232) but keeps the plate on screen. Every other pill goes through changed() (app.js:216), which clears the shown plate and offers "Plan my plates".
   - The copy says "New plates follow it" but does not say how to get new plates.
   - A client who moves the slider to 9 ("Very hungry") still sees the same 560 kcal plate above.
   - Fix: after a change, show a "Plan lunch for this appetite" button under the slider, or treat appetite like the other pills.
   - Criteria: B5, B6.
3. MINOR. At 130 % text the slider's top line squeezes into three narrow columns: "A / little", "6 of 10 · about / 480 kcal", "Very / hungry".
   - Evidence: 360-light-big/plate-dial-open-8.png (the slider frame).
   - "A little" breaks across two lines and the value line wraps.
   - Fix: put the value on its own line above the slider, and "A little" and "Very hungry" under the two ends.
   - Criteria: A9 spirit, B3.

Not inspected:
- Dragging the slider by touch, including whether the 44 px track is easy to grab one-handed.
- Whether a plate re-made at a new appetite matches the "about 480 kcal" shown (the mock plate is fixed at 560 kcal).

## 11 Plate: swap a part of the plate (plate-swap-open)

Purpose: the client swaps the main protein or the grain base for another option, and the figures follow.

Verdict: findings.

1. MINOR. The option marked as current does not match the food on the plate.
   - Under "Main protein" the selected option is "Malai paneer tikka", but "What to serve" lists "Paneer tikka, charred" (360-light/plate-swap-open-3.png).
   - The app shows whatever name Gemini gives option 0 (src/v6/app.js:299), and the prompt only asks that it be "the one already in the meal" (src/v6/engine.js:221).
   - A client cannot tell which dish is on the plate now, or whether tapping the black pill changes anything.
   - Fix: show option 0 with the plate item's own name and add "(on your plate)".
   - Criteria: B5, B7.
2. MINOR. Swaps show only a protein change, as in "(-4 g protein)". Base swaps show no change at all.
   - Evidence: "Hand-pounded brown basmati" has no figure beside it (src/v6/app.js:299). The fixture's version has 4 g less fibre.
   - With diabetes, the change in carbs and kcal is what a base swap is about.
   - Fix: show the largest change in plain words, such as "(+8 g carbs)" or "(4 g less fibre)", using the figures the swap already carries.
   - Criterion: B6.
3. MINOR. "Base" is a cook's or app's word.
   - Fix: "Grain" (with diabetes the base is always a grain) or "Rice, roti or millet". The label is at src/v6/app.js:298.
   - Criterion: B7.
4. MINOR. At 130 % text, two-line options are cramped: the words almost touch the pill's border.
   - Evidence: "Sprouted moong and tofu / bhurji (-4 g protein)" and "Hand-pounded brown / basmati" (360-light-big/plate-swap-open-4.png).
   - Fix: more vertical padding and line height on wrapped `.choice` buttons, or left-align wrapped labels.
   - Criteria: B2, B3.
5. COSMETIC. At 360 px "(-4 g" and "protein)" break across lines inside the bracket (360-light/plate-swap-open-3.png). The minus sign is a hyphen.
   - Fix: "4 g less protein" with a non-breaking space.
6. COSMETIC. The close control reads "× Done". A cross usually means cancel, so this may read as "discard my swap".
   - Fix: "Done" alone, or a tick icon.

Not inspected:
- Choosing a swap and the plate after it (no review state shows a swap applied).
- Swaps from live Gemini.

## 12 Plate: the one-tap sugar change on (plate-one-tap-on)

Purpose: one tap makes the plate gentler on blood sugar. The figures and the note show what changed.

Verdict: findings.

1. BLOCKER. After "Gentler on sugar", the client is told to serve the old amount of grain.
   - The figures change: carbs go from 44 g to 28 g, kcal from 560 to 515, and fit from 90 to 96.
   - The note says "More sprouted green moong in the khichdi, less millet, so the starch drops."
   - The serving list still says "200 g Foxtail millet khichdi", and the portion legend still says "Grains and starch 200 g" (360-light/plate-one-tap-on-3.png and plate-one-tap-on-1.png; same at 320 and at 130 %).
   - Cause: the model sends grain_grams_after (160 g in the fixture). It is checked and then dropped from the record (src/v5/core.js:557-558). viewOf changes only the totals, never the items (src/v6/app.js:107).
   - Effect: a client with diabetes who serves by the grams eats the original plate. "I'll have this" then logs 28 g carbs and 515 kcal (app.js:315), so the day's record understates what was eaten.
   - This is wrong guidance on the screen built for blood sugar.
   - Smallest fix: keep grain_grams_after on the stored change, and in viewOf set the grain item's grams to it and mark it "changed" (as swaps do). Where a change adds a food, list it.
   - Until then, do not show a one-tap change whose served grams the app cannot state.
   - Criteria: B6, B11, owner rule 5 (no invented figures).
2. MINOR. The "on" state is shown by colour only: the button turns from clay to olive, with no tick and no "On".
   - Nothing says that tapping again undoes it (360-light/plate-one-tap-on-2.png; src/v6/app.js:285-287).
   - Fix: add a tick icon to a pressed button and a short line "Tap again to undo" under the note.
   - Criteria: B5, B8 spirit.
3. COSMETIC. "10-minute version" wraps to two lines at 360 px, away from its icon. At 130 % all four labels wrap, with each icon left alone at the left edge.
   - Evidence: 360-light/plate-one-tap-on-2.png and 360-light-big/plate-one-tap-on-4.png.
   - Fix: icon above the label, or shorter labels ("10 minutes").

Not inspected:
- The other three one-tap changes when on.
- Two changes on together.
- Live Gemini notes.

## 13 Plate: eaten, logged (plate-logged)

Purpose: confirms lunch was eaten from the plates and offers the next meal.

Verdict: findings.

1. MINOR. "Change" removes the logged meal and reopens lunch (src/v6/app.js:253, which clears the meal and shows the toast "Lunch reopened.").
   - "Change" does not say that, and a client may fear it edits the dish.
   - Fix: "Undo" or "Not this, reopen lunch".
   - Criterion: B7.
2. MINOR. The primary button "Plan snack" leads to a target of "about 40 kcal and 5 g protein" (360-light/plate-logged-2.png, the Today line).
   - A 40 kcal plate means nothing to a client. Dinner is the meal that matters next.
   - Fix: when what is left for the next meal is very small, offer the meal after it ("Plan dinner") and say in one plain line that little is left for a snack. The wording and the threshold are for the owner to set.
   - Criteria: B5, B6.
3. MINOR. At 130 % text the Today line splits figures from their units.
   - Evidence: "75 / g protein", "40 / kcal" (360-light-big/plate-logged-2.png; the text comes from directive(), src/v6/engine.js:137).
   - Fix: a non-breaking space between every figure and its unit in the directive, as the tiles already do.
   - Criterion: B3 (the round 6 rule "a figure never breaks from its unit").
4. COSMETIC. There is a gap of about 50 px between the dish name and "≈ 560 kcal · 38 g protein" (360-light/plate-logged-1.png).
   - The figures float away from the dish they belong to.
   - Fix: reduce the dish heading's bottom margin in .logged.

Not inspected:
- Logging after a swap or a one-tap change (see 12.1: the log records the changed figures).
- Screen-reader announcement of the card.

## 14 Plate: the short confirmation line (toast)

Purpose: a 2.8 s line confirming the meal was saved.

Verdict: findings.

1. MINOR. The line covers the next actions and blocks taps while it shows.
   - At 320 × 568 it sits over "Change" and "Plan snack" (320-light/toast-1.png, 320-light/plate-logged-1.png).
   - At 130 % text it covers "≈ 560 kcal · 38 g protein" (360-light-big/toast-1.png).
   - It has no `pointer-events:none`, so a tap there does nothing for those seconds.
   - Fix: `pointer-events:none` on .toast. Better, show the confirmation inside the logged card, which already says "Lunch · from your plates".
   - Criteria: A3 spirit, B5.
2. COSMETIC. The text is bold italic white on near-black (the v4 rule `.toast{font-style:italic}`). Italic is harder to read for weaker near vision, and this is the only dark block in the look.
   - Fix: upright text.
3. COSMETIC. "The rest of the day follows it" is abstract.
   - Fix: "Saved for lunch. Your next meals adjust to it."

Not inspected: how long the line stays on a physical phone, and whether 2.8 s is enough for these readers. The timing was read from the code, not watched.

## 15 Plate: meal skipped while on medicines (plate-skipped-meds)

Purpose: records a skipped meal and warns a client on insulin or sugar tablets that skipping can drop their sugar.

Verdict: findings.

1. BLOCKER, the same cause as 09.1 with one fix. The warning on this screen appears only when height, weight, age, sex and activity are all set (src/v6/app.js:251, app.js:318, src/v6/params.js:56).
   - The review state seeds full body data, so it shows the warning. The same client without body data gets no warning.
   - Fix as in 09.1.
2. MAJOR. The safety warning looks like fine print.
   - It is 15-16 px grey italic (`.logged .tsub`, src/v6/extra.css). Its style matches the reassuring line before it, "The rest of the day adjusts. No meal is asked to make up for it in one sitting.", and it comes second (360-light/plate-skipped-meds-1.png).
   - The diabetes grain note on the plate screens gets an info icon, upright ink and a paper panel. This warning, which matters more, gets less.
   - For a client with weaker near vision, the reassurance reads first and the risk reads as a footnote.
   - Fix: put the warning first in the card, in the .note style (info icon, upright, ink). Keep the reassurance after it.
   - Criteria: B1, B3, B6.
3. MINOR. The warning toast holds a 25-word sentence for 2.8 s.
   - The v6 wrapper passes a "warning" flag (app.js:606), but the shared v5 toast ignores it and always closes after 2800 ms (src/v5/app.js:222).
   - At 320 px it also covers the card's text and both buttons (320-light/plate-skipped-meds-1.png).
   - Fix: let a warning toast stay until it is tapped away, or drop it, since the card holds the same words once 15.2 is fixed.
   - Criteria: B6, WCAG 2.2.1 spirit.
4. MINOR. The warning ends with "Follow your doctor's advice" and gives no step for now.
   - Fix: ask the owner for one approved next-step line, for example what to watch for and when to eat. It must come from the owner or a clinician, not be invented here.
   - Criterion: B6.
5. COSMETIC. The meal tab reads "Lunch –". The dash is a cryptic mark, and screen readers read it as "Lunch dash".
   - The card also says "skipped" twice: the caption "Lunch · skipped" and the title "Skipped lunch".
   - Fix: "Lunch, skipped" as the tab's spoken name, and drop the caption word.

Not inspected:
- Whether a snack target of "about 110 kcal" after a skipped lunch on insulin is what the coach wants. This is engine policy, not the screen.
- Live text.

## 16 Plate: made from what is at home (plate-asked)

Purpose: the client types what they have at home, and the plate answers that ask, quoting it at the top with a way back.

Verdict: findings.

1. MINOR. After "I'll have this" on an asked plate, the logged card never appears.
   - The condition `s&&!asked` (src/v6/app.js:340) keeps the plate on screen with "I'll have this" still active. Only the toast and the tick on the meal tab say it was saved.
   - This differs from screen 13. A client may tap it again or not believe it saved.
   - Fix: show the logged card (with "Back to what I asked"), or turn the button into a disabled "Saved for lunch ✓".
   - Criteria: B5, B9.
2. MINOR. This screen cannot show whether the answer honours the ask.
   - The fixture answers "Only eggs, spinach and bread at home" with the paneer and millet plate (360-light/plate-asked-1.png to -4.png).
   - The test profile is Vegetarian, which set-up separates from "Veg and egg", so an actual answer has to leave the eggs out and say why.
   - Fix: give the mock a reply built from the asked foods. Add a state where the ask conflicts with the food rules, so the read line that explains the conflict is reviewed.
   - Criterion: B11.
3. COSMETIC. At 320 × 568 the first view holds the quote, the read line and "Back to my plates". The plate starts at the fold (320-light/plate-asked-1.png).
   - This is acceptable because the answer leads. Keep the read line to two lines on small phones.

Not inspected:
- Live Gemini answers to typed asks: variety, diet conflicts and the read line.
- The wait state while the ask is made (plate-loading, another batch).

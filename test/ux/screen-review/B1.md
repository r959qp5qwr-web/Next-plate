# Screen review, batch B1 (screens 01 to 08), build 0b4f9482

Method: each screen read frame by frame at 360-light, then 320-light, 360-light-big (130 % text) and 360-dark, against the source
(src/v6/app.js, src/v5/markup.html, src/v6/legible.css, plus src/v6/engine.js and src/v5/core.js where the screen's text comes from)
and docs/ux/UX-CRITERIA.md part B. Paths below are under test/ux/out/screens/. Screenshots are 2x; pixel positions are given in CSS px.
Checks done by pixel diff: 360-dark is pixel-identical to 360-light for screens 01 to 06 and 08. On 07 the only differences are
anti-aliasing (at most 59/255 on 1,018 pixels), so the paper look holds in dark mode (A8, D2). Screen 02 is pixel-identical to screen 01
at every viewport. Screen 07 is identical to screen 06 at 320-light and 360-light-big; at 360-light it differs only by anti-aliasing.
The dish names, Pair it lines and "Why this plate" text come from the test mock (test/v5/fixture.js, test/v5/mock-gemini.ts:
"Layout sample ..."), so they are not judged as content. What the app does with them is judged.

## 01 Set-up 1 of 3: goal and food (setup-1-goal-food)

Purpose: on first open, ask for the person's goal, what they eat, foods to leave out and their region's home food.

Verdict: 4 MINOR, 3 COSMETIC.

1. MINOR. Next stays disabled with no reason given. The step-2 screen says "Answer the medicine question to go on." This step
   shows only a faded clay button (opacity .5), which looks broken to a first-time user who has answered one question but not the
   other. Evidence: 360-light/setup-1-goal-food-2.png, 320-light/setup-1-goal-food-2.png; app.js:178-180 (the hint exists only for
   step 1 with diabetes). B5, B6. Fix: until both are answered, show "Choose a goal and what you eat to go on." above the button, as on step 2.
2. MINOR. "Home food" comes already set to North. A South, West or East client who skims past it keeps North, and every plate
   follows that region. This is the only question on the screen with an answer chosen for the person. Evidence:
   360-light/setup-1-goal-food-2.png (North filled black); app.js:9 `region:"n"`. B5. Fix: start with no region chosen and make it
   required, or ask "Where is your home food from?" with no default.
3. MINOR. The intro says "Three short steps, asked once and kept on this phone." The answers are stored on the phone, but every plate
   request sends the whole profile to the server (app.js:43 profileOut, app.js:81), and the goal and health condition go into the
   Gemini prompt (core.js:599, "- Diabetes: every breakfast ..."). The only line about Gemini is the italic footnote at the bottom of
   the screen. Step 3 repeats "Kept on this phone." next to age, height and weight. Evidence: 360-light/setup-1-goal-food-1.png,
   360-light/setup-3-body-1.png. B12, B7 (truthful). Fix: "Kept on this phone. Your choices, not your name, are used to plan each plate
   with Google Gemini."
4. MINOR, applies to screens 01 to 08. The footnote carries the medical disclaimer and the Gemini disclosure, and the intro lines
   carry the promise. Both use `.fine`: italic, weight 400, 15 px, grey (legible.css:17). This is the hardest text in the app to read
   for weaker near vision, and it holds the two lines that matter for trust. Evidence: 360-light/setup-1-goal-food-2.png (footnote),
   320-light/setup-1-goal-food-1.png (intro). B3. Fix: set the disclaimer upright, 16 px, in ink2. Keep italic only for decoration.
5. COSMETIC. "Leave out completely" (choose any number) and "Home food" (choose one) use the same pill, so nothing tells the reader
   they can pick several. Evidence: 360-light/setup-1-goal-food-2.png. B5. Fix: "Leave out completely (choose any)".
6. COSMETIC. The goal tiles have icons and the diet tiles do not, so the labels start at two left edges (58 px and 28 px from the tile
   edge). Evidence: 360-light/setup-1-goal-food-1.png. B2. Fix: give the diet tiles icons, or drop the icons from the goal tiles.
7. COSMETIC, applies to every screen. The masthead tagline "your daily plate, journalled" is a second tagline beside the brand line
   "Next Plate. Made for you.", and "journalled" is not plain English for these readers. Evidence: every 360-light frame. B7. Fix: drop
   it, or use the brand line.

Not inspected: the tile states after a tap (selected goal and diet tiles are not in this state), the screen-reader order, how the
setup-hero card animates, and the 390 and 412 px widths.

## 02 Set-up 1 of 3, opened from the coach's link (setup-from-link)

Purpose: the same first step, reached from the WhatsApp link that carries the coach's code. The code is stored and cleared from the
address bar.

Verdict: carries all of screen 01's findings (pixel-identical at every viewport), plus 1 COSMETIC.

1. COSMETIC. Nothing confirms that the coach's link worked. Someone who opens the page without the link later reads "Open the link
   your coach sent you on WhatsApp once. After that, this phone remembers it." This screen never says that it has remembered.
   Evidence: 360-light/setup-from-link-1.png equals setup-1-goal-food-1.png; app.js:31. B6. Fix: one plain line under the intro,
   "Your coach's link is saved on this phone." No names.

Not inspected: the reload check (that is a measured check in review.js, not something a screenshot can show), and opening the link
inside WhatsApp's own in-app browser, where localStorage may be cleared.

## 03 Set-up 2 of 3: health (setup-2-health)

Purpose: ask for one health condition (and, with diabetes, the medicines) and a nutrient to watch, so plates stay safe.

Verdict: 1 BLOCKER, 3 MINOR, 1 COSMETIC.

1. BLOCKER. A person can pick only one health condition, so the safety referral can be skipped. "Anything on the health side?" is a
   single-choice group (app.js:134 role radiogroup, app.js:154 `P.health=v`; core.js:7). A middle-aged client with diabetes and a
   kidney condition (common) taps "Diabetes", and so does a pregnant woman with gestational diabetes. Either gets full meal plans,
   because the referral checks only `P.health==="kidney"||P.health==="preg"` (app.js:40, engine.js:579, core.js:718). That breaks owner
   rule 3: kidney condition and pregnancy are referred, never planned. The screen does not warn about this. The same limit means a
   client with diabetes and high BP loses one set of rules: the BP note and salt rules (app.js:362) or the grain rule. Evidence:
   360-light/setup-2-health-1.png (Diabetes selected, Kidney condition and Pregnant shown as other choices). B6, owner rule 3, A14.
   Smallest fix: ask kidney condition and pregnancy/nursing as their own yes/no questions for everyone (or make the group
   multi-select). Refer when either is yes, whatever else is chosen, and keep the diabetes and BP rules together when both are chosen.
   As a stopgap, show above the tiles: "Kidney condition, or pregnant or nursing? Choose that, even if you also have diabetes or BP."
2. MINOR. Choosing Kidney condition or Pregnant still sends the person through the body step to a button labelled "Show my plate",
   which then shows no plate (app.js:175-181 then blockedCard, app.js:187). The person types age, height and weight for nothing and
   is let down at the end. B6. Fix: when either is chosen, show the referral line on this step and change the button to "Done", skipping
   step 3.
3. MINOR. Two labels use jargon. "Minding any nutrient?" ("minding" here is not plain English for these readers) and "Calcium and D"
   ("D" on its own means nothing). Evidence: 360-light/setup-2-health-1.png; engine.js:6. B7. Fix: "Want more of a nutrient?" and
   "Calcium and vitamin D".
4. MINOR. The health condition is sent into the Gemini prompt (core.js:599). Where it is chosen, the screen gives only the general
   footnote. See 01-3. B12. Fix: as in 01-3.
5. COSMETIC. "No, neither" wraps to two lines inside its pill at 320 px and at 130 % text, with the text almost touching the border.
   Evidence: 320-light/setup-2-health-1.png, 360-light-big/setup-2-health-2.png. B2. Fix: "No" (the question is yes/no), or
   let that pill take more width.

Not inspected: the Yes and Not sure states, the kidney and pregnancy picks on this step (the referral cards belong to another batch),
and whether clients on metformin understand "sugar tablets such as glimepiride or gliclazide" (answering Yes is the safe side).

## 04 Set-up 3 of 3: body (setup-3-body)

Purpose: ask for sex, age, height, weight and activity, so each meal gets a calorie and protein target.

Verdict: 1 MAJOR, 3 MINOR.

1. MAJOR. Height is asked only in cm, and a wrong value is wiped without a word. Most Indian adults know their height in feet and
   inches. Typing "5.4" or "64" is out of range (120-215) and the field is quietly emptied on change (app.js:165
   `if(!P[x[0]])i.value=""`), with no message. The person can tap "Show my plate" without noticing, and then every plate comes with
   no calorie target. Weight and age behave the same way. Evidence: 360-light/setup-3-body-1.png (Height box, placeholder "cm" only).
   B6, IX-36. Fix: accept feet and inches (two small boxes, or a cm/ft switch). When a value is out of range, keep it and say why
   under the box: "Height in cm, for example 160."
2. MINOR. Nothing says this step can be skipped, or what happens if it is. "Show my plate" works with every box empty, and the
   plate then has no targets (state plate-no-body). Evidence: 360-light/setup-3-body-1.png. B6. Fix: under the heading, "You can skip
   this. Without it, plates have no calorie target."
3. MINOR. The boxes do not look like boxes to type in. They are borderless surface-colour cards like the tiles, and the unit is only
   the placeholder, so after typing "72" the "kg" disappears. Evidence: 360-light/setup-3-body-1.png, 320-light/setup-3-body-1.png.
   B5. Fix: a visible 1 px ink border, and the unit as a fixed suffix inside the box.
4. MINOR. The "Activity" label sits about 10 px under the input boxes, while every other group has 22 px or more above it, so it reads
   as a caption of the inputs. Evidence: 360-light/setup-3-body-1.png (inputs end at about y 428, "Activity" at about y 439),
   320-light/setup-3-body-2.png. B2. Fix: give the Activity group the same top margin as `.qs`.

Not inspected: the screen with values typed in or with an invalid value (no state for either), the numeric keypad that opens, and
what VoiceOver/TalkBack read for the inputs' labels.

## 05 Plate: making the plate (plate-loading)

Purpose: after set-up or on opening, show that today's plate for this meal is being made.

Verdict: 4 MINOR, 3 COSMETIC.

1. MINOR. Controls stay live while the plate is being made, and taps on them are dropped without a word. The four example asks
   and the "Type a craving or a dish" box look ready, but `makePlates` returns at once while busy (app.js:79 `if(UI.busy)return;`,
   app.js:332). The typed text stays in the box and nothing happens. Evidence: 360-light/plate-loading-1.png,
   360-light/plate-loading-3.png. Brief Q5, B5. Fix: disable the send button and prompts while busy, with "Wait for this plate first",
   or keep the ask and send it once the plate arrives.
2. MINOR. The only sign of progress is three animated dots after the italic line "Planning your lunch plates", for a request that
   may take up to 95 s (app.js:73). A first-time 50-year-old sees an empty plate drawing and may think it is stuck. Evidence:
   360-light/plate-loading-1.png. B6. Fix: add "This takes about 20 seconds." (the measured typical time). After 30 s, add a line that it
   is still working.
3. MINOR, applies to screens 05 to 08. The example asks do not fit these clients or the time of day: "Heavy leg day: a high-protein
   dinner" and "Trek tomorrow: food to carry" (sedentary, pain-prone clients), a dinner ask at lunch, and "What to order at a restaurant
   tonight", which belongs to Rescue (app.js:321). Evidence: 360-light/plate-loading-3.png. B7. Fix: everyday asks such as "Only dal,
   rice and curd at home", "A light dinner after a heavy lunch", "Something for a sweet craving".
4. MINOR, applies to screens 05 to 08. At 130 % text the Today line separates figures from their units ("75" at the end of one line,
   "g protein" on the next, and "480" / "kcal"). Part A round 6 fixed that a figure stays on one line with its unit. Evidence:
   360-light-big/plate-loading-2.png; engine.js:139-142 uses ordinary spaces. A4/B3. Fix: join the figure and unit with   in
   `directive()`.
5. COSMETIC. At 130 % text the rim words "BREAKFAST" and "LUNCH" run into each other. This is ornament (D5), but visibly broken.
   Evidence: 360-light-big/plate-loading-1.png. Fix: set the rim text in px, not scaled with the text setting, or space it by arc length.
6. COSMETIC, applies to screens 05 to 08. "planned around: ..." starts in lower case (sentence case). Evidence:
   360-light/plate-loading-1.png. B7. Fix: "Planned around: ...".
7. COSMETIC. "Lunch carries about 480 kcal" ("carries" is an odd verb here). Evidence: 360-light/plate-loading-2.png; engine.js:142.
   B7. Fix: "Lunch: about 480 kcal and 31 g protein".

Not inspected: the animation itself (stills only; the dots differ between light and dark captures at 549,917), switching meal tabs
while a plate is loading, and the real wait time on a 4G phone.

## 06 Plate: the plate for this meal (plate-hero)

Purpose: show this meal's plate (the plate drawing, grams per food group, the dish, nutrients, what to serve) and let the person take it,
change it or log something else.

Verdict: 1 MAJOR, 6 MINOR, 3 COSMETIC.

1. MAJOR. The one-tap changes look like main actions, and their on/off state is shown only by colour. When off they are filled clay
   with white text, the same style as "I'll have this" (legible.css:100-101). When on they turn olive (legible.css:102) with no tick and
   no change of words. The person meets four filled main-action-style buttons before the one that matters, and cannot tell whether a
   change is on except by remembering the colours. Evidence: 360-light/plate-hero-2.png (four clay buttons), compared with
   360-light/plate-one-tap-on-2.png (one olive); 320-light/plate-hero-3.png. B5 (one primary action per state; state visible), B8.
   Fix: off as an outline (like "Next plate"), on as filled with a tick, and "On" added to the accessible name.
2. MINOR. One plate shows two different sets of grams in the same colours: the legend "Protein foods 150 g" (weight of food) and the
   tile "Protein 38 g" (nutrient), and likewise "Grains and starch 200 g" against "Carbs 44 g". A reader who weighs food can take the
   wrong figure. Evidence: 360-light/plate-hero-1.png and -2.png. B1, B7. Fix: head the tiles "In this plate" and the legend "Weigh out",
   or put the legend grams only in "What to serve".
3. MINOR. "Fit 90 / 100" and "Rise: Gentle" are not explained. Fit to what? Rise of what? The owner's band is the after-meal rise in
   blood sugar, and "Rise" alone does not say that. Evidence: 360-light/plate-hero-1.png (chip), 360-light/plate-hero-2.png (tile).
   B7. Fix: "Sugar rise after the meal: Gentle" and "Close to your target: 90 / 100", or drop Fit.
4. MINOR. "Try it" opens the Builder (app.js:313), but it reads like "try eating it". Evidence: 360-light/plate-hero-4.png. B7. Fix:
   "Change the mix" or "Open in Builder".
5. MINOR, applies to screens 05 to 08. The Your day tiles and the setting pills sit in sideways rows with the scrollbar hidden
   (built CSS `.tiles,.rail{overflow-x:auto;scrollbar-width:none}`). At 360 px the Fibre tile and the Appetite, cuisine, mood, cooking
   and Minding pills are off screen, with only a cut edge to hint at them. The appetite dial changes this meal's calorie target.
   Evidence: 360-light/plate-hero-6.png, 360-light-big/plate-hero-8.png. B1, B5. Fix: wrap the pills onto two lines, and the tiles into a
   3-column grid (the studio layout already does this).
6. MINOR, applies to screens 05 to 08. The fixed header (with the food chip), the ask bar and the tab bar take about 265 of 740 px at
   360 and about 220 of 568 px at 320, so the plate is read through about 60 % of the screen. Evidence: 360-light/plate-hero-1.png (header
   ends at about y 125, ask bar starts at about y 600), 320-light/plate-hero-1.png (header ends at about y 93, ask bar starts at about y 442).
   B1, A3. Fix: let the food chip scroll away with the masthead, and show the ask bar only at the end of the page or behind a button.
7. MINOR, applies to screens 05 to 08. The only way to change food rules, health or body is the "Vegetarian · North ⌄" line in
   the header, and nothing says it opens My food. Evidence: 360-light/plate-hero-1.png. B5. Fix: "My food: Vegetarian · North ⌄", or
   "Change" after it.
8. COSMETIC. The dot ornament under the dish title is 64 px wide on a 12 px repeat, so a sixth dot shows as a cut-off sliver. Evidence:
   360-light/plate-hero-2.png (right of the five dots). Built CSS `.hero h2::after{width:64px ... 0 0/12px 7px}`. Fix: width 60px.
9. COSMETIC. "Pair it" puts a "+" (app.js:308) before "Tea or coffee, an hour later", which is advice to keep tea and coffee away from
   the meal. Evidence: 360-light/plate-hero-4.png. Fix: an "after" or clock mark for timing pairs, or no glyph.
10. COSMETIC. When a one-tap label wraps, the icon stays pinned left and the two lines are centred, so the label drifts from its icon.
   Evidence: 320-light/plate-hero-3.png ("10-minute version", "Easy on the gut"), 360-light-big/plate-hero-3.png. B2. Fix: left-align
   the label beside the icon.

Not inspected: live Gemini dishes, Pair it and "Why this plate" text (mock "Layout sample" here), the Swap drawer and logged states
(other batches), how slowly the plate arc draws, and whether clients read the ring colours without the legend (the legend is there, B8 holds).

## 07 Plate: a plate kept from earlier today (plate-old-saved)

Purpose: when the app is reopened the same day, show the plate kept on the phone. A plate kept under the old grain rules is
dropped and made again.

Verdict: carries all of screen 06's findings (the rendered screen is the same plate), plus 1 MINOR.

1. MINOR. The kept plate is dropped and replaced with no word to the person (app.js:25-27). With live Gemini the new plate will
   usually be a different dish. A client who saw a plate in the morning, and may have bought for it, finds another one with nothing to
   say why, and no way back to the old one. Evidence: 360-light/plate-old-saved-1.png is the fresh plate; there is no note in any
   frame. B6. Fix: once, after the replacement, a note above the plate: "Plates were updated after an app change, so this plate is
   new." Short and dismissible.

Not inspected: whether the loading card flashes before the new plate (the capture shows only the end state), and what a person sees
when the kept plate is still valid (the ordinary kept-plate case has no state of its own).

## 08 Plate: two foods left out (plate-leave-two)

Purpose: show a plate when dairy and nuts are left out. The food-rule chips must stay readable and not be cut off.

Verdict: carries screen 06's findings, plus 1 MAJOR, 1 MINOR, 2 COSMETIC.

1. MAJOR. A "No nuts" plate is labelled "NUTTY". The taste line reads "SAVOURY · NUTTY" just under the "No nuts" chip. Taste words are
   screened for health words, claims and banned words, but not against the person's left-out foods (engine.js:282-286 `tasteWords`
   does not call `crosses`). A client who left out nuts, often for an allergy, will either distrust the plate or think nuts are in it.
   "Creamy", "buttery" or "cheesy" on a no-dairy plate, and "eggy" on a vegetarian one, would get through the same way. Evidence:
   360-light/plate-leave-two-1.png, 320-light/plate-leave-two-1.png; fixture taste ["savoury","nutty","light"] (test/v5/fixture.js:36),
   where "light" is dropped but "nutty" is shown. B7, B11, owner rule 5 spirit (no misleading text). Fix: drop any taste word that names
   or suggests a left-out food (nutty with nuts; creamy, buttery, cheesy, milky with dairy; eggy with veg or vegan), and add these to the
   rule check.
2. MINOR. Three dead one-tap buttons stay on screen in pale clay with white text (about 2:1, hard to read for weaker near vision),
   plus a line saying they are not offered. Evidence: 360-light/plate-leave-two-2.png, 360-light-big/plate-leave-two-3.png;
   app.js:285-288. B5, brief Q7 (dead controls cost attention). Fix: show only the changes that are offered, and keep the one "Not
   offered for this plate: ..." line in readable ink.
3. COSMETIC. The not-offered note breaks "10-minute" at its hyphen across lines at 320 px. Evidence:
   320-light/plate-leave-two-3.png. Fix: a non-breaking hyphen (U+2011) in "10-minute".
4. COSMETIC. At 130 % text the chips wrap so that "No nuts" shares a line with "Fit 96 / 100" and "10 min". Food rules and plate facts
   mix. Evidence: 360-light-big/plate-leave-two-1.png. B2. Fix: food-rule chips on one line, Fit and time on the next.

Not inspected: plates with three or four foods left out (longer chip rows), a live plate that actually contains a left-out food (the
plate check is in the engine and is not visible in a screenshot), and Swap options under these rules.

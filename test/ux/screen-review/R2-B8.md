## 113 Menu, diabetes: a different menu after a No (menu-grain-other)
Purpose: after a No on one menu, the person reads a different menu. The question is asked again and no earlier answer is shown.

Verdict: 2 MINOR, 2 COSMETIC.
1. MINOR (B9 one look, B7 plain words). The order card labels the band tile "Rise" (360-light/menu-grain-other-1.png;
   320-light/menu-grain-other-2.png; app.js:568 `cell("Rise",band,...)`). The plate's tile was renamed "Sugar rise" because "Rise" alone was
   unexplained (FIXES B1 06-3, app.js:333). The fix did not reach menu orders, so the same band has two names in the app. The person reading a
   menu is the one with diabetes, where the band matters most.
   Smallest fix: label the order tile "Sugar rise" as well (app.js:568). This also applies to 114, 115's earlier order, and 116.
2. MINOR (B3 legible at arm's length, B12). The footnote under the order is 6 to 7 lines of 15 px grey italic (`.fine`, legible.css:17;
   app.js:609): "Read as: ... Estimates for how this kind of place usually serves it. Kitchens vary. 2 dishes on this menu did not pass your
   food rules, so they are not shown. The menu is read by AI (Google Gemini). ..." (360-light/menu-grain-other-3.png; at 130 %,
   360-light-big/menu-grain-other-4.png). It carries two things the person needs: that dishes were hidden, and that Gemini read the menu. The
   same kind of notice on set-up and Plate was raised to upright 16 px ink2 for this audience (FIXES B1 01-4, `.fine.disc` at legible.css:250).
   Smallest fix: give this footnote the `disc` class (upright, 16 px, ink2). This also applies to 114 and 116.
3. COSMETIC (B2). Under "Also on this menu" at 320 px, the line breaks before a separator, so one line starts with "· ≈ 23 g protein", and
   "80 / 100" sits alone on the last line (320-light/menu-grain-other-3.png). At 130 % the next line starts "≈ 520 kcal" after a dangling "≈ 80 g
   ·" (360-light-big/menu-grain-other-3.png). Smallest fix: join each separator to the word before it with a no-break space ("kcal ·"),
   and keep "target:" with its figure (app.js:597).
4. COSMETIC (B2). At 130 % text, the first common-dish chip is wider than the phone. Its label is cut at the edge ("2 butter naar"), and no
   second chip shows to hint that the row scrolls (360-light-big/menu-grain-other-5.png; the same on 115, 360-light-big/menu-grain-yes-then-no-2.png).
   A1 allows a horizontal scroller, but here the chip's own words are cut. Smallest fix: let a chip wrap to two lines up to about 85 % of
   the width.
- Good: the order leads, the question box is clear, Yes and No carry equal weight and are 48 px tall, and the grain shows its grams
  ("1 Tandoori Roti, ≈ 40 g"). Nothing is clipped at 320 px or 130 %.
- Fixture text, not raised: "heavy for a dinner" under lunch (MOCK-ONLY, FIXES B5 40).
Not inspected: in this state the "different menu" is the same menu plus "Lassi 80" (review.js:172), so the screen cannot show a really

## 114 Menu, diabetes: two grains in one order (menu-grain-two)

Verdict: 1 MINOR, 1 COSMETIC (plus 113-1 and 113-2).
1. MINOR (screen book; owner rule 2 as decided: a grain side shows its count and its grams). The screen book shows "with Steamed Rice and
   1 Roti" and, under "Also on this menu", "with 1 Roti", with no grams (360-light/menu-grain-two-1.png, -2.png). A client would not see

   engine (review.js:175-177, 261-263, 269-271). The owner reads the screen book to approve screens, and here it contradicts his own decision.

   "Change answer" link (app.js:581). The same line gets one once both grains are answered (app.js:589-590). A rice Yes tapped by mistake can
   only be undone after the roti question is answered. Smallest fix: add the same link at app.js:581.
- Good: the first answer stays on screen above the second question (51-1 fixed). The question box, Yes and No fit at 320 px and at 130 %

## 115 Menu, diabetes: Yes to one grain, No to the other (menu-grain-yes-then-no)
Purpose: after Yes to steamed rice and No to roti, every order needs roti, so a note says no order is left and what to do next.

Verdict: 1 MINOR, 1 COSMETIC.
1. MINOR (B6, a truthful next step). The note reads: "You said steamed rice is available, but roti is not available, and every order Next
   Plate picked from this menu needs it. Ask which roti or rice they have, then name your dish with it." (360-light/menu-grain-yes-then-no-1.png;
   app.js:534). The person has just confirmed that rice is available, and the note tells them to ask which rice they have. To a client this
   reads as if the app did not take in their answer. It also does not say the one step that clearly works: name a dish with the rice they
   confirmed. Smallest fix: when a Yes is on record, end the note with the confirmed grain, for example "... needs roti. Name your dish with
   steamed rice instead." Keep the present wording when there is no Yes.
2. COSMETIC (B7). The first sentence chains three clauses ("..., but ..., and ...") over 27 words, and at 130 % it runs to 7 lines before
   any control (360-light-big/menu-grain-yes-then-no-1.png). Smallest fix: two sentences: "You said roti is not available. Every order Next
   Plate picked from this menu needs it."
- Good: the note leads at every width. "Change answer" and the "Name the dish" button sit inside it, and the button is 44 px or more.
  "Read the menu" is outlined. The clay rule marks the note as a problem, as the owner decided.
Not inspected: where focus lands, and the live-region text (app.js:540); the keyboard rising after "Name the dish".

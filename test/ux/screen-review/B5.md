## 38 Menu: typing a menu (rescue-menu-typed)
Purpose: the person pastes or types a restaurant menu and asks Next Plate to read it for this meal.

Verdict: 2 MINOR, 3 COSMETIC.

1. MINOR. At 320 px the main action is below the fold. The 7-row box (app.js:556 `ta.rows=7`; extra.css:25 `min-height:176px`) pushes "Read this menu for lunch" under the tab bar in the first view (320-light/rescue-menu-typed-1.png). It is only reached by scrolling (320-light/rescue-menu-typed-2.png). With the phone keyboard up it is further away still. A client who has just pasted a menu one-handed does not see what to tap next.
   Smallest fix: at heights of 600 px or less, use 4 rows / about 110 px for the box, or keep the submit button in view under the box (position: sticky; bottom above the tabs).
2. MINOR (truthful against the source). The guide line under every menu entry state says "Before you order, read the menu: you get one order for your lunch, and the reason." (app.js:575). What comes back (screen 40) is one order, two more under "Also on this menu", a leave list and a tip. No sentence gives "the reason"; the nearest thing is "planned around: …". It promises something the screen does not show.
   Evidence: 360-light/rescue-menu-typed-2.png and 360-light/rescue-menu-order-1..4.png.
   Smallest fix: "Read the menu: you get the order that fits your lunch best, and two others."
3. COSMETIC. There is a lot of repeated reading. One screen has three Gemini notices ("The photo goes…", "The menu goes…", "What you type goes…"). The guide paragraph then repeats "Already chosen? Name the dish", which is the label just above it, and it sits below the controls it explains (360-light/rescue-menu-typed-2.png, 320-light/rescue-menu-typed-3.png).
   Smallest fix: hide the photo note while the menu box is open, and hide the guide paragraph once the box is open (app.js:549, 575).
4. COSMETIC. The "or type the menu" link has no underline and keeps the same label while the box is open. It is also the only way to close the box, which nothing on screen shows. In the same clay italic it looks like the non-tappable "Ask: …" line on screen 40 (360-light/rescue-menu-typed-1.png; app.js:547; legible.css:69, 107).
   Smallest fix: underline it (as was done for "Change answer" in round 31). While open, label it "Close the typed menu" or "Hide the box".
5. COSMETIC. The box opens scrolled to the end: "STARTERS … MAINS" are hidden and there is no sign that more lines sit above. This is a box that scrolls inside a page that also scrolls. At 130 % the last visible line ("Gulab Jamun 90") is cut at the bottom edge (360-light-big/rescue-menu-typed-2.png, -3.png).
   Smallest fix: let the box grow with its text up to a cap (`field-sizing: content; max-height: 50vh`).

Not inspected: the layout with the on-screen keyboard up; a real paste from WhatsApp (long lines, emoji); 390/412 px.

## 39 Menu: reading the menu (menu-wait)
Purpose: tells the person that the menu is being read and that they should wait.

Verdict: 2 MINOR, 1 COSMETIC.

1. MINOR. During the read, controls stay live but do nothing. readMenu() sets `UI.mopen=false` (app.js:452), so the camera button turns back to the filled clay primary (app.js:544). It becomes the strongest control on the screen while the person is meant to wait (360-light/menu-wait-1.png, 320-light/menu-wait-1.png). The dish chips, the "Already chosen" send button and the camera all look ready. Tapping them does nothing: rescue() and readMenu() return early when busy (app.js:440, 451). A photo picked during the wait is thrown away without a word.
   Smallest fix: while `UI.mbusy`, keep the camera button outlined, hide the chip row, and mark the camera and the send button `disabled` (or aria-disabled with a dimmed style).
2. MINOR. The wait says nothing about how long it takes and offers no way out. The request can run for up to 95 s (app.js:73). The typed menu disappears from the screen during the wait (app.js:452), so the person cannot see what was sent, and may think it was lost.
   Evidence: 360-light/menu-wait-2.png.
   Smallest fix: add one line under "Reading the menu for your lunch", such as "This can take up to a minute. Your menu stays here if it doesn't work."
3. COSMETIC. At 320 px the animated dots wrap onto a line of their own under "lunch" (320-light/menu-wait-2.png, -3.png).
   Smallest fix: join the dots to the last word (a no-break space before them, or `white-space:nowrap` on the last word and the dots).

Not inspected: where the screen lands after the tap (frames start at the top; the review's in-view check passed); the animation itself; the photo path to this wait.

## 40 Menu: what to order (rescue-menu-order)
Purpose: shows the one order that fits this meal, with grams, kcal, protein and rise band, the grain question (diabetes), two other orders, what to leave and how to eat it.

Verdict: 3 MINOR, 2 COSMETIC.

1. MINOR (hierarchy, B1/B5). The answer does not lead. After a successful read, `UI.mopen` is false, so "Read the menu" (start over) is again the filled clay primary, and the eight-dish chip row sits between it and the order. In the first frame the order card only begins at the bottom edge (360-light/rescue-menu-order-1.png). After "Yes" there are two clay primaries on one screen: "Read the menu" and "I'll order this" (app.js:502, 544). The page scrolls to the card (revealOut), but once the person scrolls up, the strongest control is the one that throws the answer away.
   Smallest fix: while `UI.mres` is shown, draw the camera button outlined (`UI.mopen||UI.mres` at app.js:544) and leave out the chip row (app.js:561-564).
2. MINOR (diabetes; truthful against the source). With diabetes, the sweet is taken out of "Leave for another day". The leave list drops any line that crosses the person's food rules (engine.js:570, `!bad(l.name+" "+l.why)`). badWords() adds SWEETS for diabetes (core.js:486; "gulab jamun" is in SWEETS, core.js:79). So the fixture's Gulab Jamun line never shows, and only Malai Kofta is listed (360-light/rescue-menu-order-3.png). The one dish on this menu a diabetic client most needs to hear about gets no mention. The list then reads as if everything else on the menu were fine.
   Smallest fix: keep diet crossings out of the leave list (meat for a vegetarian), but with diabetes show sweets on the menu as "Leave for another day" with a fixed reason, such as "a sweet; with diabetes, keep sweets for another day". Alternatively, filter the leave list on diet words only, not SWEETS.
3. MINOR (owner rule 2, grams and kcal). The grain side is shown only as a count: "with 1 Tandoori Roti", and "with 2 Tandoori Roti" under "Also on this menu". The main portion is shown in grams ("6 pieces, ≈ 180 g"). The engine already asks the model for the grain's grams (engine.js:364, `grain_grams`), but the card does not show them (app.js:495, 530).
   Evidence: 360-light/rescue-menu-order-2.png, -3.png.
   Smallest fix: when the side is the grain and grain_grams is declared, show "with 1 Tandoori Roti (≈ 40 g)"; otherwise leave it as it is, without inventing a figure.
4. COSMETIC (fixture only, but it is what the owner sees in the review). On a lunch read the leave reason says "heavy for a dinner" (360-light/rescue-menu-order-3.png). "2 Tandoori Roti" should be plural.
   Smallest fix: in test/v6/fixture-menu.js, use a reason that fits any meal ("Fried koftas in a cream gravy, a heavy dish") and "2 Tandoori Rotis".
5. COSMETIC. "Ask: Go easy on the butter brushed on top" uses the same clay italic as the tappable "or type the menu", so it can read as a link. The fine print "the checks and the ranking are done by code" is builder language (360-light/rescue-menu-order-4.png; app.js:496, 537).
   Smallest fix: draw "Ask:" in ink with a bold "Ask the waiter:" label, and replace the fine print with "Next Plate checks the dishes against your food rules and ranks them."

What holds: kcal and protein add up (380 + 120 = 500 kcal; 24 + 4 = 28 g). Rise is a band ("Gentle"), never mg/dL. The grain question shows with diabetes, with Yes and No of equal weight (owner decision, round 28-29). "I'll order this" stays hidden until Yes. Everything fits at 320 px and at 130 %, with no clipping (320-light/rescue-menu-order-2..5.png, 360-light-big/rescue-menu-order-2..5.png). Tap targets look 44 px or more.

Not inspected: the screens after Yes and after No (other batch); live Gemini variety in the leave reasons, the tip and the ask lines; whether a client reads "Fit 92 / 100" correctly.

## 41 Menu: nothing on it fits (menu-nothing-fits)
Purpose: tells the person that no dish on the menu they sent fits their food rules, and what to do instead.

Verdict: 2 MINOR, 2 COSMETIC.

1. MINOR (plain words, B7). "No dish on that menu passed the checks for you." (app.js:457) is vague. "The checks" is a word no client knows, and the screen does not say why. Screen 40 calls the same thing "your food rules".
   Evidence: 360-light/menu-nothing-fits-2.png.
   Smallest fix: "None of the dishes on that menu fits your food rules. Try another page of the menu, or name the dish you want above."
2. MINOR (applies to 41-44). The note sits in the wrong place. It is drawn after the "Already chosen? Name the dish" form (app.js:565-572), so it is cut off from the menu box it is about. It is followed by the guide paragraph "Before you order, read the menu: …" (app.js:575), which repeats the first-visit guidance right after a failure.
   Evidence: 360-light/menu-nothing-fits-2.png; 320-light/menu-nothing-fits-3.png.
   Smallest fix: draw the menu note inside `.menu-in`, straight under the menu box or camera button, and skip the guide paragraph whenever a note shows.
3. COSMETIC. Without focus, the menu box has no border (extra.css:25, `border:0`), and its last visible line is cut through the middle ("Palak Paneer 300"). It looks like a clipped card of text, not a box you can edit (360-light/menu-nothing-fits-1.png, 320-light/menu-nothing-fits-2.png).
   Smallest fix: give it a 1 px ink-3 border, and a bottom padding of whole lines so no line shows half cut.
4. COSMETIC (B9). The error note uses the same torn tan paper as the "How to eat it" tip on screen 40, so a problem and a piece of advice look alike (compare 360-light/menu-nothing-fits-2.png with 360-light/rescue-menu-order-3.png).
   Smallest fix: give `.note` the surface colour with a clay rule on the left, and keep the tan paper for tips.

Not inspected: a live menu where every dish is refused (the state uses an empty orders reply); the screen-reader reading of the note.

## 42 Menu: photo could not be read (menu-unreadable)
Purpose: tells the person that the menu photo could not be read, and to take a clearer photo or type the menu.

Verdict: 1 MINOR, 1 COSMETIC.

1. MINOR (B6, next step). The note names two next steps, "Try a clearer photo, or type the dishes", but the note itself holds no control for either. The page scrolls the note into view (revealOut), and at 320 px the camera button and the "or type the menu" link are then off screen above it (320-light/menu-unreadable-2.png). A dish chip row sits between them (360-light/menu-unreadable-1.png). No "Try again" is correct here (A13).
   Smallest fix: add "Take another photo" (opens #mfile) and "Type the menu" (opens the box) inside the note, as "Try again" sits inside the other notes (app.js:571).
2. COSMETIC. The note says "type the dishes" and the link says "or type the menu" (app.js:457, 547).
   Smallest fix: "Try a clearer photo, or type the menu."

Not inspected: a real camera photo from a phone (the state uses eval/v6/menu-blurred.jpg through the test server); the file picker on Android and iOS.

## 43 Menu: menu not understood (menu-unparsed)
Purpose: tells the person that the reply came back broken, and offers Try again.

Verdict: 2 MINOR, 1 COSMETIC.

1. MINOR (plain words, B7). "The answer came back cut off." (app.js:60, ERRS.unparsed) is technical and can be read the wrong way. A client is likely to think their menu was cut off and start retyping it. It is the service's reply that broke.
   Evidence: 360-light/menu-unparsed-2.png.
   Smallest fix: "Next Plate couldn't finish reading the menu. Try again." The same string serves the log and Rescue, so use a wording that fits all three, such as "Next Plate's reply came back broken. Try again."
2. MINOR (B5, one primary). Two buttons do the same thing. "Read this menu for lunch" (filled clay primary, app.js:558) and "Try again" (outlined, inside the note, app.js:571) both send the same menu again. The one the note points to is the weaker-looking one, and a client may wonder whether they differ.
   Evidence: 360-light/menu-unparsed-2.png, 320-light/menu-unparsed-2.png.
   Smallest fix: while a retry note shows, draw "Read this menu" outlined and "Try again" as the primary. Do not remove Try again; A13 requires it.
3. COSMETIC. "Try again." in the text and then a "Try again" button repeats itself.
   Smallest fix: end the sentence at the cause when a button follows.

Not inspected: how often this happens live; the screen-reader reading.

## 44 Menu: could not reach (menu-error)
Purpose: tells the person that Next Plate could not get an answer, keeps the typed menu, and offers Try again.

Verdict: 2 MINOR.

1. MINOR (B5). Same as 43.2: "Read this menu for lunch" as the filled primary and "Try again" as the outlined control both resend the same menu (360-light/menu-error-2.png; app.js:558, 571).
   Smallest fix: as in 43.2.
2. MINOR (accessibility, applies to 41-44). The error is not announced. The menu note is a plain div with no role and no live text (app.js:568). Nothing is written to the polite live region #said (markup.html:20), and focus stays on the submit button. The grain "No" path does announce (app.js:474-475). A client using TalkBack, or anyone with enlarged text whose screen does not land on the note, gets no word that the read failed.
   Smallest fix: after render, write UI.merr to $("said").

What holds: the wording "Next Plate couldn't get an answer just now. Try again." is plain and honest, the typed menu stays in its box (A13), and Try again is offered. Everything fits at 320 px and at 130 % (320-light/menu-error-3.png, 360-light-big/menu-error-2.png, -3.png).

Not inspected: a real network loss on a phone (the state fakes a 502); the 95 s timeout path.

# Screen review B4: screens 29 to 37 (build 0b4f9482)

Method: for each screen I opened every frame at 360-light, then 320-light and 360-light-big (130 % text), and looked at 360-dark. I read the
source in src/v6/app.js (renderBuild 386-427, rescue 439-448, renderRescue 539-601), src/v6/legible.css, src/v4/shell.html (base styles
built into the page by build6.js), src/v5/core.js (checkRescue 579-591) and the review fixtures (test/ux/review.js, test/v5/fixture.js).
I did not run the review: the screenshots are taken as current for this build, as the brief says.
Every screen in this batch uses the test profile "Vegetarian · North", lunch.

Dark mode: on all nine screens 360-dark matches 360-light pixel for pixel in look (paper, ink, clay). D2 holds. I found nothing that applies to dark mode only.

## 29 Builder: build the plate by parts (builder)
Purpose: set how much of the plate goes to protein, vegetables, carbs and fats, then plan this meal with that plate.

Verdict: findings

1. MINOR. The screen does not say what it is for, and its numbers are unexplained. Under the title "Build the plate" the first things are four
   preset pills (Desk day, Balanced, Post-training, Steady sugar) and four rows of percentages. Nothing says these are shares of the plate,
   and the engine reads them as "by volume" (src/v6/engine.js:201). Next to "480 kcal" in the ring, a reader can take "36%" as a share of the
   calories. "Slow carbs" is diet jargon. The label does not say "grain", which is the word that matters with diabetes.
   Evidence: 360-light/builder-1.png; src/v6/app.js:388, 401.
   Smallest fix: one line under the title: "Choose how much of the plate each part takes. Then plan your meal with it." Add "of the plate" to
   the ring's caption, and rename "Slow carbs" to "Grains (roti, rice, millet)".
2. MINOR. The one action, "Plan lunch with this plate", is below the fold at 360 and 320. Frame 1 shows only presets, the ring and two rows.
   Evidence: 360-light/builder-1.png (button first seen in builder-2.png at y≈745); 320-light frames 1-2.
   Smallest fix: make the ring smaller on short screens, or repeat the action as a sticky button above the tab bar.
3. MINOR. The curve legend "with your habits" and "carbs first, sitting after" is unclear. With no habit ticked the two lines lie almost on
   top of each other, and "carbs first, sitting after" reads as an instruction, not as the comparison line.
   Evidence: 360-light/builder-3.png; src/v6/app.js:420.
   Smallest fix: "with the habits you ticked" and "if you eat carbs first and sit after". Hide the second line when no habit is ticked.
4. MINOR (applies to every screen in this batch). The masthead, the "Vegetarian · North" pill and the tab bar stay fixed and take about
   200 of the 740 px (27 %) on every frame. At 130 % text they take about 217 px. Each scrolled frame shows only about 480 px of content.
   Evidence: 360-light/builder-1.png (header to y=250 of 1480 image px, tabs from y=1335); 360-light-big/builder-2.png.
   Smallest fix: let the masthead (date and tagline line) scroll away, and keep only the slim brand line and the tabs fixed.
5. COSMETIC. The habit tiles are uneven. "15-minute walk" pushes its small line to the bottom of the tile and leaves a gap. The third tile sits
   alone in a two-column grid. Evidence: 360-light/builder-3.png.
6. COSMETIC. At 130 % text the ring's centre fills the hole: "480" touches the ring at the top and "protein" touches it at the bottom.
   Evidence: 360-light-big/builder-1.png (ring crop).
See also 30-1 (MAJOR): the curve and its caption on this screen claim to move with the plate's carbs, protein and fat, and they do not.

Not inspected: a profile with diabetes. I could not see what happens when "Slow carbs" is set to 5 % or "Steady sugar" (10 % carbs) is used
and the server's 30 g grain rule then refuses the plate. A habit tile in its ticked state is also unseen: no review state ticks one. The
style exists in src/v4/shell.html:543. I could not judge the screen-reader reading of the ring.

## 30 Builder: greens changed (builder-adjusted)
Purpose: show the plate shares after the person changes one part.

Verdict: findings

1. MAJOR. The after-meal curve on the same screen ignores the person's changes, yet its caption says it follows them. After greens +10
   (40→50 %, carbs 12→8 %), the curve, its band ("Gentle") and its title are the same as before. The curve is drawn from the plate already
   planned (curMeal/viewOf), not from the shares above it (src/v6/app.js:413-416). The caption right below it says "It moves with carbs,
   fibre, protein and fat, and with the habits above" (app.js:426). A client who taps carbs − to steady the curve sees nothing move and
   concludes the change did nothing, or that the curve is broken.
   Evidence: 360-light/builder-2.png and 360-light/builder-adjusted-2.png have the same curve and the same "Gentle".
   Smallest fix: title the card "After-meal curve of your current plate", and change the caption to "It shows the plate planned now. Plan
   with your new plate to see its curve." Or move the curve card under a "Your current plate" heading, apart from the steppers.
2. MINOR. Nothing on screen says the change is not used yet. After the taps, the only signs are new percentages and no preset selected.
   The plate is not re-planned until "Plan lunch with this plate", which is below the fold (360-light/builder-adjusted-1.png).
   Smallest fix: after a stepper tap, show one line above the ring: "Changed. Tap Plan lunch with this plate to use it."
Findings 29-1, 29-2 and 29-4 apply here too (not counted again).

Not inspected: the steppers at their 5 % and 65 % limits, and the − and + buttons at those limits (they stay enabled; the tap does nothing).

## 31 Builder: no plate yet (builder-no-plate)
Purpose: Builder when no plate could be made; the shares can still be set and planned.

Verdict: CLEAN (apart from 29-1, 29-2 and 29-4, which apply here and are counted at 29).
"Plan a plate first. Its curve shows here." is honest, and the button just above it does that. The target "480 kcal · 31 g protein" comes
from the phone, not the failed server call, so it is correct to show it. 320-light frame 3 and 360-light-big frames 2-3 hold.

Not inspected: what the person sees after tapping "Plan lunch with this plate" while the service is still failing. That is the Plate error
state, in another batch.

## 32 Rescue: start (rescue-entry)
Purpose: eating out. Photograph or type a menu to get one order, or name a dish already chosen to get moves that make it fit.

Verdict: findings

1. MINOR. The explanation comes last, in the smallest, faintest text. The only plain account of what Rescue does, and the useful figures
   "850 kcal and 66 g protein are left today", are at the bottom, below the controls, in 15 px grey italic (.fine, legible.css:17). It also
   repeats "Already chosen? Name the dish" from the label above. A 50-year-old reads the controls before knowing what they give.
   Evidence: 360-light/rescue-entry-1.png (paragraph at y≈1150-1285, partly under the tab bar); 320-light/rescue-entry-2.png.
   Smallest fix: move one short line under the title at body size: "850 kcal and 66 g protein are left today. Photograph the menu for one
   order that fits, or name the dish you chose." Drop the bottom paragraph.
2. MINOR. The dish chips have no label, and one tap sends at once. "Paneer butter masala with 2 butter naan", "Chole…" and the rest sit under
   "for example, chole bhature" with no heading, so they read as examples. A tap sends the dish to Gemini straight away (app.js:562) and uses
   one of the day's requests. The pressed state set in code (aria-pressed) has no style: the base style is for .sit[aria-checked] only
   (src/v4/shell.html:88, 483). After a tap, the chip shows nothing.
   Smallest fix: a caption "Or tap a common dish" above the row, and a style for .sit[aria-pressed="true"].
3. COSMETIC. Alignment is mixed. "At a restaurant or a function" and "Already chosen?" are left-aligned; the photo note and "or type the
   menu" are centred (legible.css:174). Evidence: 360-light/rescue-entry-1.png.
4. COSMETIC. At 130 % text the active-tab underline cuts through the bottom of the letters of "Rescue".
   Evidence: 360-light-big/rescue-limit-1.png (tab bar crop); the same on every 360-light-big Rescue frame.
5. COSMETIC. The title "Eating out anyway" can read as a scolding to a client who eats out for a function or for work. "Eating out" alone says
   the same.

Not inspected: the camera or file picker that "Read the menu" opens on a real Android or iPhone. The menu flow after "or type the menu" is in
another batch.

## 33 Rescue: working on a craving (rescue-wait)
Purpose: show that the named dish is being worked out.

Verdict: findings

1. MINOR. Controls stay live during the wait but do nothing. "Read the menu" keeps its primary clay look, and the send button and the dish
   chips stay enabled. While the wait runs, rescue() and readMenu() return without a word (app.js:440, 451). A client who photographs the
   menu during the wait loses the photo silently.
   Evidence: 360-light/rescue-wait-1.png (all entry controls look live above the wait card).
   Smallest fix: disable the send button, the chips and "Read the menu" (aria-disabled, dimmed) while the wait runs, or queue the photo.
2. MINOR. The wait line uses an odd phrase and gives no time. "Taking apart masala dosa with chutney" is not plain English for these readers.
   The line does not say how long it takes, and the call may run up to 95 s (app.js:73).
   Smallest fix: "Working out masala dosa with chutney. This takes up to a minute." (app.js:567).
3. COSMETIC. The animated dots wrap onto a line of their own at 360 ("..", 360-light/rescue-wait-1.png). The cause is the inline-block
   ::after in src/v4/shell.html:226. A non-breaking join, or a min-width of 0 on the last word, would fix it.
4. COSMETIC. This wait card is plain text. The menu wait uses the plate wait card with its drawing, so the two Rescue waits look different.

Not inspected: whether a screen reader announces the wait. The line is not written to the #said live region.

## 34 Rescue: the answer to a craving (rescue-result)
Purpose: show the named dish's energy and macros, and the moves that bring it closer to the day's plan, then log it.

Verdict: findings

1. MAJOR. The heading "Moves that make it fit" sits over a move that makes it fit worse, and the app ticks that move for the person. The first
   move is pre-ticked (app.js:443). Here it is "Eat a bowl of cucumber-pudina raita first, +65 kcal". With it ticked the screen shows every
   figure going up (Energy 1120 ↑ 1185 kcal, Carbs ↑ 122 g, Fats ↑ 54 g) and "Fit 20 → 12 / 100", worse. The heading still says the moves
   make it fit (app.js:592). "I had this, for lunch" then logs 1185 kcal, raita included, though the person never chose the raita (app.js:598).
   Only 850 kcal are left today, and nothing says so. Most clients will read the ticked move as the advice, and the falling Fit as a fault.
   Evidence: 360-light/rescue-result-2.png and rescue-result-3.png; the same at 320 and at 130 %.
   Smallest fix: do not pre-tick moves (start with UI.rmoves={}). Title the list "Moves you can make". Under the comparison, say plainly
   when a move adds energy ("adds 65 kcal; softens the rise").
2. MINOR. The comparison has no legend, and its score is unexplained. Each row has two bars, a light one and a dark one, with nothing to say
   which is before and which after. "Fit 20 → 12 / 100" is a score defined nowhere on this screen, and it sits in 15 px grey italic.
   Evidence: 360-light/rescue-result-2.png.
   Smallest fix: a caption "As served → with your moves" over the bars, and "Fit to your day: 20 of 100" in words.
3. MINOR. A move is dropped because it names the person's own dish. The fixture's portion move "Set aside a third of the rice, keep all the
   mutton" (−310 kcal), the only move that lowers the energy, was removed because "mutton" crosses the vegetarian profile (src/v5/core.js:587,
   crosses(h.title,p)). The dish name itself is exempt, because the person typed it. The same will happen in live use. A person who leaves
   out dairy and names paneer butter masala loses any move that names the paneer, so only moves that add food are left.
   Evidence: test/v5/fixture.js:44 against 360-light/rescue-result-3.png (two moves shown of four; the psyllium move was rightly dropped as a
   supplement).
   Smallest fix: in checkRescue, do not apply crosses() to words the person typed in the dish name.
4. MINOR (fidelity of the review state). The state shows text no client would see, so this screen could not be fully judged. The review
   types "Mutton biryani, full handi" for a Vegetarian profile (test/ux/review.js:77). The fixture's breakdown shows on screen as "Layout
   sample from your Gemini file, not AI output." (test/v5/fixture.js:42). Its portion "Full handi, as delivered" has no grams.
   Smallest fix: give the review a vegetarian dish with a portion in grams and a breakdown written as a client would see it.
5. MINOR. At 130 % text the Energy row breaks in two: "1120 ↑" on one line, "1185 kcal" on the next, with the arrow left hanging. The other rows
   stay on one line. Evidence: 360-light-big/rescue-result-2.png.
   Smallest fix: keep the struck figure, the arrow and the new figure together (nowrap), or put the struck figure on the line above.

Not inspected: a live Gemini answer (wording, portion in grams, number of moves); a reply with status "refer"; the screen after "I had this"
(the Plate toast, in another batch); the move tiles' pressed style when the walk is ticked.

## 35 Rescue: service busy (rescue-busy)
Purpose: tell the person the service is busy and let them try again.

Verdict: findings

1. MINOR. The message and the button disagree. The message says "Try again in a minute.", and a "Try again" button sits under it, ready at
   once. A client taps at once and likely gets "busy" again.
   Evidence: 360-light/rescue-busy-2.png; app.js:60 (ERRS.busy), 569.
   Smallest fix: "Next Plate is busy just now. Wait a minute, then tap Try again."
2. COSMETIC. The bottom explanation ("Before you order, read the menu…") comes back under every error note and adds a third block of text to
   an error screen. Evidence: 360-light/rescue-busy-2.png.
What works: the typed dish stays in its box, Try again keeps it, and the note is in the Raw Linen look.

Not inspected: the screen after "Try again" while still busy; the screen-reader reading of the note (it is not in a live region).

## 36 Rescue: today's limit (rescue-limit)
Purpose: tell the person that the day's Rescue requests are used up, and when they come back.

Verdict: findings

1. MAJOR. A client at the restaurant gets no next step for this meal. The note says "That's today's limit for Next Plate. It opens again at
   5:30 am." That gives the reopening time, and nothing to do now. Rescue is used at the table. "Read the menu" stays the primary clay button,
   the send button stays live, and the paragraph below still says "Before you order, read the menu". Each of these will hit the same limit:
   a photo taken now is sent and refused. A13 is met (no Try again), but B6 is not: the screen does not say what to do next.
   Evidence: 360-light/rescue-limit-1.png and -2.png; app.js:67-69, 544, 575.
   Smallest fix: (a) at the limit, dim "Read the menu" and the send button and hide the bottom paragraph; (b) add a next step that needs no
   new figure, for example "For this meal, choose the dish with the most vegetables and protein, or message your coach on WhatsApp." (text
   for the owner to approve).
2. MINOR. "Today's limit" assumes the client knows there is one. Nothing earlier tells them that requests per day are limited, so the first
   meeting with the limit is at the moment of need.
   Smallest fix: "You have used today's requests for Next Plate. They open again at 5:30 am."

Not inspected: limit_ip ("on this phone"), shared by several people on one network; the time shown on a phone outside India.

## 37 Rescue: could not reach (rescue-error)
Purpose: tell the person the answer did not come, and let them try again.

Verdict: findings

1. COSMETIC. "Try again" appears twice, as the last sentence of the note and as the button under it ("Next Plate couldn't get an answer just
   now. Try again." with [Try again]). Evidence: 360-light/rescue-error-2.png. Smallest fix: end the sentence at "just now."
2. COSMETIC. The bottom paragraph repeats under the note (as 35-2).
What works: the text is honest, Try again is offered and keeps the typed dish (A13), and the layout holds at 320 and 130 %.

Not inspected: the network-failure wording ("Couldn't reach Next Plate just now. Check your signal and try again."), which is the true "could
not reach" case. The review fires a 502 upstream error, so that wording is never rendered.

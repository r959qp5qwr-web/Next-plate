## Version 2 (about 250 words, about 90 seconds)

1. You already know what healthy food looks like. Where most people get stuck is at two in the afternoon, hungry, trying to decide what to eat right now.
2. A diet chart doesn't help much there. It doesn't know you skipped breakfast, or that lunch was at a wedding, or that dinner is going to be at ten.
3. So I built Next Plate. Before a meal you open it, and it tells you what to eat, based on how your day has actually gone.
4. You get one plate. Normal home food, quantities in grams, and a line on why it picked that for you today.
5. If you ended up eating something else, just type what you had. Nobody scolds you. It counts it and adjusts the rest of your day.
6. At a restaurant, take a photo of the menu and it tells you what to order. And if the chole bhature has already arrived, it shows you how to make that work too.
7. It remembers your week, so if you've had rajma three nights in a row, tonight will be something else.
8. Your numbers come from your own age, height, weight and goal, and you can see exactly how they were worked out.
9. It also knows where to stop. With diabetes, it keeps the grain on your plate and measures it. And if something needs your doctor, it says so and suggests nothing.
10. That's all it is, really. One less thing to think about, three times a day.
11. If you'd like to try it, message me and I'll send you your link.

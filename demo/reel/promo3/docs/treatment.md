# Next Plate: film treatment v3 ("One decision")

Reference: Apple's product films (iPhone and Apple Watch feature films). What they do, and what v2 did not:
- One idea per shot, held long enough to read twice. A shot lasts 4 to 8 seconds, never under 3.
- The device is the hero: large, centred, still or drifting slowly. The screen is shown at a size you can read.
- App interactions at their own speed: a tap, the screen changes, a card arrives. Nothing is drawn over the UI.
- When a detail matters, the camera moves in to it (a slow push to the order card), instead of a pen circling it.
- Type is sparse: one short line at a time, set large, in soft ink on paper. It fades in and holds; it never bounces.
- Music: felt piano, soft strings, a low pulse. Calm and warm. Silence is allowed.
- Voice: calm and close, as if speaking to one person. Long pauses.

Structure (about 55 s, 9:16):
| # | beat | picture | line on screen | voice |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | open | paper, then a single line | Three times a day. | Three times a day, you decide what to eat. |
| 2 | promise | the phone rises into frame, Plate tab | One decision, made easy. | Next Plate makes that one decision easy. |
| 3 | menu | Rescue tab: tap Read the menu, the menu goes in, Reading..., Your order appears; slow push in to Palak Paneer | Show it the menu. | At a restaurant, show it the menu. It picks the dish that suits your day so far. |

| 6 | care | push in to "the grain stays (diabetes)" and the 110 g grain row | It keeps your roti. | And with diabetes... |
| 7 | close | phone settles; NEXT PLATE masthead; "a dietitian in your pocket"; Message me for your link | | Next Plate. A dietitian in your pocket. Message me... |

Look: a Raw Linen paper backdrop (#F2ECE1), ink type in EB Garamond, one clay accent. No colour fields, no numerals, no pen marks.
Proof first: a 15 s test of shots 1 to 3 for the owner's eye before the rest is built.
